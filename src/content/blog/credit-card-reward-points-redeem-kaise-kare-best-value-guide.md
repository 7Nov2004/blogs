## ⚠️ Reward Redemption Fee Se Kaise Bachein?

Ek hidden charge jo sabhi banks lagate hain:
> **Reward Redemption Processing Fee: ₹99 + 18% GST = ₹116.82**

Aap chahe 500 points redeem karein ya 50,000 points redeem karein, ye ₹117 ki fee har transaction par lagegi!

### Golden Rule:
Kabhi bhi 2,000 se kam points redeem na karein. Agar aap ₹250 ka voucher lene ke liye 1,000 points redeem karte hain aur uspe ₹117 bank fee lag jati hai, toh aapke hath mein aadhi value hi aayegi. Hamesha saal mein **sirf ek baar bulk points** redeem karein.

## Co-Branded Cards vs Core Reward Cards: Kaunsa Better Hai?

Reward optimization ke liye sahi card structure samajhna bohot helpful hai:

- **Direct Cashback Cards:** Agar aapko points calculate karne ya expire hone ka jhanjhat bilkul nahi chahiye, toh Amazon Pay ICICI, Airtel Axis, ya SBI Cashback jaise cards sabse best hain jahan har mahine direct rupee credit milta hai.
- **Core Reward Points Cards:** Agar aap saal mein 1 ya 2 baar family ke sath travel karte hain, toh HDFC Regalia Gold, Infinia ya Axis Atlas jaisi premium reward currencies par flight aur hotel redemption se 3x se 5x zyada value nikal sakti hai.
