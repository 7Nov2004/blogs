Kya aapke smartphone par bhi har dusre din **"Storage Space Running Out"** ka notification aa jata hai? Jab camera kholo toh photo nahi khichti, ya Play Store se koi naya app download nahi hota? 

Sabse pehle log apni purani ya yaadein bhari photos aur videos delete karne lagte hain. Lekin aapko aisa karne ki bilkul zaroorat nahi hai! Aaj hum aapko 5 aisi **hidden settings** batayenge jisse bina ek bhi photo delete kiye aapke phone mein 10 se 15GB space turant ban jayegi.

## 1. WhatsApp Ka Hidden Media Cache Saaf Karein

Hamare phone ki aadhe se zyada storage WhatsApp ki un-necessary files kha jati hain (jo groups mein aane wale good morning videos ya duplicate memes hote hain).

### Kaise Saaf Karein:
1. WhatsApp open karein aur top-right 3 dots par click karke **Settings** mein jayein.
2. **Storage and Data** ➔ **Manage Storage** par tap karein.
3. Wahan aapko **"Larger than 5 MB"** ka option dikhega. Wahan badi-badi bekar videos ko ek sath select all karke delete kar dein.
4. Sath hi kisi purane group ka data jo kaam ka nahi hai, use wahi se clear kar lein. Is akele step se 4-5GB space bach jati hai.

## 2. Telegram Ka Cache Size Limit Set Karein

Agar aap movies, PDFs ya web series dekhne ke liye Telegram use karte hain, toh Telegram aapke bina bataye phone mein 10GB tak ka cache bhar deta hai!

### Setting Badlein:
1. Telegram Settings ➔ **Data and Storage** ➔ **Storage Usage** mein jayein.
2. Wahan **"Clear Entire Cache"** par tap karein (Dhyan rahe: Isse aapke chat messages delete nahi honge, sirf downloaded temporary files saaf hongi).
3. Neeche ek slider hoga **"Keep Media"** — usko "Always" se hata kar **"3 Days"** ya **"1 Week"** par set kar dein. Iske baad Telegram khud purana kachra saaf karta rahega!

## 3. Chrome Aur Social Apps Ka Cache Data Clear Karein

Instagram, Facebook, YouTube aur Chrome browser rozana browse kiye gaye web pages ka data jama karte rehte hain.

* Phone ki **Settings ➔ Apps** mein jayein.
* Instagram, Chrome, aur YouTube ko open karein.
* **Storage & Cache** par tap karein aur **"Clear Cache"** par click karein.
* *(Note: "Clear Data" par click mat karna, sirf "Clear Cache" dabana taaki aapka login na hate).*

## 4. Google Photos Ka "Free Up Space" Magic Button

Agar aapki photos Google Photos app par backup ho rahi hain, toh wo photos aapke phone ki memory mein bhi padi rehti hain aur cloud par bhi.

* **Google Photos** app open karein.
* Apni profile icon (top right) par click karein.
* Wahan **"Free up space on this device"** ka button dikhega.
* Is par click karte hi wo photos jo already Google cloud par safe hain, wo phone ki internal memory se delete ho jayengi. Photos aapke Google Photos mein hamesha rahengi, par phone mein 5GB se zyada memory khali ho jayegi!

## 5. Gallery Ka "Trash / Recycle Bin" Check Karein

Bohot se log photos delete toh kar dete hain, par unhe pata nahi hota ki Android mein delete ki gayi photos 30 din tak **Recycle Bin** ya **Trash** folder mein padi rehti hain aur storage gher ke rakhti hain.
* Phone ki Gallery / File Manager kholein.
* "Recently Deleted" ya "Trash" folder par jayein aur **"Empty Trash"** kar dein.
