## YouTube AI Monetization Rules (2026 Updated Policy)

YouTube ne automated spam channels par strict action lena shuru kiya hai. In 3 baaton ka hamesha dhyan rakhein:

1. **Avoid 100% Repetitive Content:** Har video mein different visual angles aur fresh script honi chahiye.
2. **Human Touch is Mandatory:** AI voiceover ke sath captions ke timing aur editing cuts par manual effort dikhna chahiye.
3. **Synthetic Media Disclosure:** Video upload karte waqt 'Altered Content' checkbox par zaroor tick karein agar visuals realistic insaan jaise dikhte hain.
