## College Laptop Kharidte Waqt Ye 4 Baatein Zaroor Check Karein

### 1. SSD is 100% Mandatory (Never Buy HDD)
Agar kisi saste laptop mein "1TB Hard Disk" likha ho, toh use galti se bhi mat lijiye. HDD par Windows 11 itni slow chalti hai ki boot hone mein 3 minute lagte hain. Hamesha **512GB NVMe M.2 SSD** wala model hi lein jo 10 seconds mein boot karta hai.

### 2. 16GB RAM vs 8GB RAM
Agar aapka budget ₹38,000-₹40,000 hai, toh pehli preference **16GB RAM** wale model ko dein. Isse aapka laptop agle 4 saal bina kisi lag ke chalega.

### 3. Anti-Glare Display
College library aur classrooms mein tube lights aur khidkiyon ka reflection screen par aata hai. Hamesha **Anti-Glare coating** wala matte display chunein taaki aankhon mein dard na ho.

### 4. MS Office Genuine License
Check karein ki laptop ke sath **Microsoft Office Home & Student (Lifetime Genuine License)** pre-installed mil raha hai ya nahi. Isse aapko Word, Excel aur PowerPoint ke liye alag se paise nahi dene padenge.
