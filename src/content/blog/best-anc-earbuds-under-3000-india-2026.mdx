## 💡 Buyer Guide: Earbuds Kharidte Waqt In 4 Mukhya Baaton Ka Dhyan Rakhein

1. **ANC Level (dB Rating):** 25dB se 30dB light commute ke liye sufficient hota hai. High noise cancellation ke liye 40dB+ rating check karein.
2. **Driver Size (10mm vs 12.4mm):** Bada driver size (12.4mm / 13mm) better air displacement karta hai jisse bass impact better milta hai.
3. **App Support:** Confirm karein ki brand ki official companion app (Realme Link, HeyMelody) milti hai ya nahi, taaki firmware updates aur touch controls customize ho sakein.
4. **Water Resistance:** Minimum **IPX4 / IP55 rating** zaroori hai taaki sweating se internal circuitry damage na ho.
