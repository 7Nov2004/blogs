## Perplexity AI Kya Hai? (The Answer Engine)

Perplexity AI traditional search engines aur Large Language Models (LLMs) ka ek perfect fusion hai. Isko **"Answer Engine"** kaha jata hai.

Jab aap Perplexity par koi sawal puchte hain (jaise: *"India mein 5G launch hone ke baad telecom companies ka ARPU kaise badha?"*):
1. Ye web par real-time search karta hai.
2. Top 10-15 reliable news articles, research papers aur data sources ko seconds mein padhta hai.
3. Aur aapke samne ek **Concise, Structured, Point-to-Point Summary Answer** likh kar rakh deta hai.
4. Sabse khaas baat: Har line ke upar chhota sa number `[1]`, `[2]` hota hai. Us par click karte hi aap direct us website par pahunch jaate hain jahan se wo data liya gaya hai!

## Perplexity AI Ke Top 5 Features Jo Isse Super-Smart Banate Hain

### 1. 🎯 Focus Modes — Targeted Research Filter
Search bar ke paas ek **"Focus"** button hota hai jo search scope ko define karta hai:
- **All:** Pure web par comprehensive search.
- **Academic:** Sirf peer-reviewed research papers (arXiv, PubMed, JSTOR) par search karta hai — students aur scholars ke liye goldmine!
- **Writing:** Bina internet search kiye pure creative writing mode (code, email, ya essays generate karne ke liye).
- **YouTube:** Specific topics par bane informative YouTube videos ke transcripts dhoondh kar summarize karta hai.
- **Reddit:** Agar aapko kisi gadget ya service ka real unfiltered user experience janna hai, toh ye direct Reddit threads ke discussions extract kar leta hai.

### 2. ⚡ Pro Search (Pehle Copilot Kaha Jata Tha)
Jab aap Pro Search on karte hain, toh Perplexity simple answer dene ke bajaye aapse clarifying questions puchta hai:
- Example: Agar aapne pucha *"Best laptop for me"*, toh ye puchega: *"Aapka budget kitna hai? Coding karni hai ya gaming? Weight kitna prefer karenge?"*
- Aur aapke specific requirements ke according perfect tailored recommendation dega.

### 3. 📂 Collections & File Uploads
Aap alag-alag topics par **Collections** bana sakte hain (jaise *"College Project 2026"*, *"Tech Blog Ideas"*). Aap direct PDF files, spreadsheets ya images upload karke unpar real-time data queries chala sakte hain.

### 4. 🤖 Multiple Flagship Models Ka Access
Perplexity Pro mein aapko kisi ek model par restrict nahi kiya jata. Settings mein jakar aap choose kar sakte hain:
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** (Natural writing aur coding ke liye)
- **GPT-4o** (General intelligence aur logic ke liye)
- **Sonar Large** (Perplexity ka apna ultra-fast search model)
