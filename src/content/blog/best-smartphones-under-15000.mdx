## 5G Smartphone Kharidne Se Pehle Ye 4 Checks Zaroor Karein

### 1. 5G Bands Check (n28 & n78)
Kuch saste phones sirf 1 ya 2 5G bands dekar bachte hain jisse indoor signal nahi aata. Hamesha confirm karein ki phone mein **minimum 8 se 12 bands** hon, jisme **n28 (700MHz indoor coverage) aur n78 (high-speed data)** compulsory hon.

### 2. Battery Capacity (5000mAh vs 6000mAh)
5G modem 4G se zyada energy consume karta hai. Agar aap din bhar 5G data on rakhte hain toh 6000mAh battery wale phones ko prefer karein. Battery settings optimize karne ke liye hamara [5G Phone Battery Drain Problem Solution](/blog/5g-phone-battery-drain-problem-solution) zaroor padhein.

### 3. Dedicated SD Card Slot vs Hybrid Slot
Agar aap 128GB storage model le rahe hain aur photos/movies save karte hain, toh check karein ki SIM tray mein 2 SIM ke sath alag se memory card lagta hai ya nahi.

### 4. Bloatware & Ads Reality Check
Xiaomi aur Realme ke phones mein installation ke waqt "Glance Lockscreen" aur "Personalized Ads" ko uncheck karna na bhoolein. Agar bilkul zero ads chahiye toh Motorola ya Samsung select karein.
