## Power Bank Lete Samay 3 Zaroori Baatein:

1. **Two-Way Fast Charging Zaroor Ho:** Bina fast input ke 20,000mAh power bank ko charge hone mein 10-12 ghante lag jate hain. 18W ya 65W input se yeh 2 se 4 ghante mein full ho jata hai.
2. **Flight Allowance:** 20,000mAh (74Wh) power banks domestic flights ke cabin bag mein 100% allowed hote hain (check-in bag mein na rakhein).
3. **Earbuds Charging Mode:** Hamesha aisa power bank chunein jisme low-current mode ho taaki TWS earbuds ya smartwatches ki chhoti batteries overheat na hon.
