## ⏱️ Pehle 10 Minute Ka Golden Rule: Official Revert Link

Jaise hi hacker aapka email badalta hai, Instagram aapke **purane original email address** par ek emergency security alert bhejta hai:

### Original Email Inbox Check Karein:
1. Apna wo email check karein jo pehle Instagram se juda tha (Spam/Junk folder bhi dekhein).
2. `security@mail.instagram.com` se aayi mail dhoondein jiska subject hoga: *"Email address changed on Instagram"*.
3. Us mail ke andar ek hyper-secure link hota hai: **"Secure your account here"** ya **"Revert this change"**.
4. Agar aap is link par click karte hain, toh hacker ka naya email turant cancel ho jata hai aur purana email automatically restore ho jata hai!

*Alert:* Agar ye mail aaye huye 24 ghante se zyada ho chuke hain ya link expire ho chuka hai, toh niche diya gaya Step-2 follow karein.

## 🔐 Hacker Ka 2FA (Two-Factor Authenticator) Kaise Hatayein?

Kayi chalak hackers account hack karne ke baad apna khud ka Google Authenticator app link kar dete hain, taaki password reset hone ke baad bhi aap login na kar sakein.

Is problem ko hatane ka official tarika:
1. Jab login karte waqt aapse 6-digit 2FA code maanga jaye, toh niche **"Try another way"** par click karein.
2. Select karein: **"Support Request"**.
3. Description box mein likhein:
   > *"My account was compromised and the hacker enabled unauthorized Two-Factor Authentication. Please revoke all active 2FA sessions."*
4. Apne Identity verification ID ke saath submit karein. Instagram team 24 ghante ke andar hacker ke saare active devices aur 2FA tokens ko forcibly disconnect kar deti hai.

## Account Wapas Aane Ke Turant Baad Ye 4 Kaam Karein

Jaise hi aapka login restore ho, 5 minute ke andar ye security protocol implement karein:

1. **Password Badlein:** Ek strong 16-character password banayein jisme symbols, numbers aur capital letters hon.
2. **Active Sessions Terminate Karein:**
   - *Settings → Accounts Center → Password and Security → Where you're logged in*.
   - Wahan jitne bhi anjaan phone ya laptop dikhein, sabko **"Log Out of All Devices"** kar dein.
3. **Authenticator App (2FA) Enable Karein:** SMS OTP ke bajaye **Google Authenticator** ya **Microsoft Authenticator** use karein. SMS OTP hacking ke samne kamzor hota hai. WhatsApp security ke liye hamara [WhatsApp Passkeys Setup Guide](/blog/whatsapp-passkeys-kya-hai-setup-guide-hindi/) check karein.
4. **Backup Codes Download Karein:** Settings mein *Two-Factor Authentication → Backup Codes* par jaakar 8-digit ke 5 emergency codes ka screenshot lekar safe drive ya diary mein note karein.
