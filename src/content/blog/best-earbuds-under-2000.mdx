## Budget Earbuds Kharidte Samay In 5 Baaton Ka Dhyan Rakhein

Dukan par ya Amazon par product images dekh kar attract hone se pehle ye technical points zaroor check karein:

### 1. Driver Size (10mm vs 12.4mm)
Driver earbud ka speaker hota hai. 12.4mm driver 10mm ke mukable zyada air push karta hai, jisse bass thumping aur soundstage wide milta hai. Hamesha 12mm+ drivers prefer karein.

### 2. Real ANC vs Marketing Gimmicks
Bohat si cheap companies box par bada-bada "Noise Cancellation" likhti hain lekin andar sirf ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation for calls) hota hai. Hamesha check karein ki box par **ANC (Active Noise Cancellation)** with dB rating (e.g. 30dB/32dB) mention ho.

### 3. Bluetooth Version (5.3 ya 5.4)
Bluetooth 5.3+ power consumption ko 30% kam karta hai aur connection stability badhata hai taaki phone pocket mein hone par sound cut-cut ke na aaye.

### 4. Codec Support (AAC vs SBC)
SBC standard basic audio codec hai. Hamesha check karein ki earbud **AAC (Advanced Audio Codec)** support karta ho, jo high-bitrate clean music deliver karta hai iPhone aur Android dono par.

### 5. IPX Rating for Gym & Workout
Sweat (paseena) earbud ke electronics ko kharab kar sakta hai. Gym use ke liye minimum **IPX4 ya IP55** rating hona zaroori hai.
