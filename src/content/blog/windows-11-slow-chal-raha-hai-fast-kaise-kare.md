Windows 11 dekhne mein bohot modern aur khoobsurat hai, lekin iske naye visual effects, background animations aur unnecessary services ki wajah se purane laptops aur budget PCs par ye kaafi slow chalne lagti hai. Boot hone mein time lagna, Chrome kholte hi fan tez chalna, ya foldering open karte waqt lag hona aam baat hai.

Agar aapka PC bhi slow ho gaya hai, toh naya laptop khareedne ya Windows format karne ki koi zaroorat nahi hai. Bas ye **5 secret settings** badal lijiye:

## 1. Startup Apps Ko Turant Disable Karein

Jab aap PC on karte hain, toh Spotify, Steam, Microsoft Teams aur Cortana jaisi 15-20 apps background mein chupke se on ho jaati hain aur RAM gher leti hain.

### Kaise Band Karein:
* Keyboard par `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` dabakar **Task Manager** kholein.
* Left side mein **Startup Apps** icon (speedometer) par click karein.
* Wahan jin apps ki aapko computer on hote hi zaroorat nahi hoti (jaise Telegram, Discord, OneDrive), unpar right-click karke **"Disable"** kar dein.
* Isse PC ka on hone ka samay (Boot time) aadhā ho jayega!

## 2. Transparency Aur Visual Effects Ko Band Karein

Windows 11 ke blurry glass effects (Transparency) graphics card aur processor par bohot load daalte hain.
* Settings ➔ **Accessibility** ➔ **Visual effects** mein jayein.
* **Transparency effects** aur **Animation effects** dono ko **Turn OFF** kar dein.
* Isse windows aur folders bina kisi delay ke instant open honge.

## 3. Storage Sense On Karke Temporary Files Hatayein

Windows rozana hazaron cache aur update files bana kar C: drive mein bhar deti hai.
* Settings ➔ **System** ➔ **Storage** mein jayein.
* **Storage Sense** ko **ON** kar dein.
* Neeche **"Temporary files"** par click karein aur wahan se Previous Windows Installations aur Temporary files ko tick karke **"Remove files"** kar dein. Isse 10-20GB SSD space instant free ho jayegi.

## 4. Power Plan Ko "Best Performance" Par Set Karein

By default Windows battery bachane ke liye processor ki speed ko limit karke rakhti hai.
* Settings ➔ **System** ➔ **Power & battery** mein jayein.
* Power mode ko "Balanced" se hata kar **"Best performance"** par set kar dein.
* *(Note: Agar laptop battery par chal raha hai toh ise balanced rakhein, charging par hamesha Best Performance use karein).*

## 5. Background Services Aur Telemetry Band Karein

* Settings ➔ **Privacy & security** ➔ **Diagnostics & feedback** mein jayein.
* "Send optional diagnostic data" ko off kar dein taaki Windows lagatar Microsoft ko data send karke internet aur CPU use na kare.

## Bonus Advice: SSD Upgrade Zaroor Karein

Agar aapke PC mein abhi bhi purani spinning Hard Drive (HDD) lagi hui hai, toh chahe aap kitni bhi settings badal lein, PC slow hi rahega. Ek sasti 256GB ya 512GB SATA SSD lagwa lijiye — aapka purana laptop bilkul naye jaisa fast chalne lagega!
