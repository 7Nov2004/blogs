## Kya Dekhna Chahiye Laptop Mein (Buying Guide)

- **Processor:** Kam se kam Intel Core i5 (13th Gen) ya AMD Ryzen 5.
- **RAM:** 2026 mein minimum 16GB RAM honi chahiye.
- **Storage:** 512GB NVMe SSD compulsory hai.
- **Display:** Full HD (1080p) IPS panel.
- **Battery:** Kam se kam 6-8 ghante ka backup.
