## Smartwatch Buying Checklist (Kya Dhyan Rakhein?)

1. **AMOLED vs TFT:** ₹3,000+ budget mein hamesha AMOLED display hi chuniye. Deep black colors aur battery saving dono milte hain.
2. **Water Resistance:** Kam se kam IP68 ya 5ATM rating honi chahiye taaki baarish ya pasine se ghadi kharab na ho.
3. **Battery Expectation:** Bluetooth calling use karne par battery 3-5 din chalti hai, calling off rakhne par 8-10 din.
