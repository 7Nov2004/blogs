## 💡 Buyer Guide: Ergonomic Chair Kharidte Waqt Ye 4 Baatein Zaroor Check Karein

1. **Adjustable Lumbar Support:** Har vyakti ki height alag hoti hai. Lumbar pad upar-neeche adjust hona zaroori hai taaki lower back curve par hi pressure aaye.
2. **Breathable Mesh vs Cushion Back:** Hamesha **Mesh Back** prefer karein taaki 8 ghante baithne par bhi back heat up na ho.
3. **Class 4 Gas Lift:** Chair ki safety aur durability ke liye hamesha **Class 4 BIFMA Certified** hydraulic system hi select karein.
4. **Seat Depth & Armrest Height:** Seating pad aisi honi chahiye ki ghutne (knees) ke pichhe 2-inch ka gap rahe aur 90-degree leg posture maintain ho.
