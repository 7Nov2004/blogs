Baarish ke mausam mein, haath dhote waqt, ya baalti mein galti se phone gir jana ek bohot aam ghatna hai. Chahe aapka smartphone IP68 water resistant hi kyun na ho, paani mein girne ke baad jab aap kisi ko call karte hain ya gaana bajate hain, toh **speaker se dabi-dabi aur fat-fat aawaz (muffled sound)** aane lagti hai.

Iska kaaran yeh hai ki paani ke microscopic drops phone ke bottom speaker grill ke fine mesh par sathe hue reh jate hain aur sound vibrations ko block kar dete hain.

Aam log aisi sthiti mein phone ko jhatakne lagte hain ya chawal (rice) ke dabbe mein daal dete hain, jo phone ko theek karne ke bajaye aur zyada kharab kar deta hai. 

Is technical guide mein hum janenge **165Hz Sonic Wave Water Eject Trick** ke baare mein jisse bina phone khole 2 minute mein speaker ka pura paani bahar nikal aata hai!

## 🚫 3 Badi Galtiyan Jo Phone Paani Mein Girne Par Kabhi Na Karein

Pehle yeh samajhna zaroori hai ki kin galtiyon se phone ka speaker permanent dead ho sakta hai:

1. **Chawal (Rice) Mein Daalna:** Chawal phone ka paani nahi sookhta. Ulta chawal ke bareek dane aur safed starch speaker ke chhedon mein phaskar use permanent jam kar dete hain.
2. **Hair Dryer Ka Hot Air Lagana:** Hair dryer ki tez garmi phone ke waterproof rubber gaskets aur display glue ko pighla deti hai, jisse paani motherboard tak pahunch jata hai (Aur baad mein display par [Green Line Issue](/blog/smartphone-green-line-issue-display-replacement-policy-india/) jaisi samasya aa sakti hai).
3. **Pin Ya Toothpick Ghusedna:** Speaker grill ke pichhe ek patli waterproof membrane hoti hai. Agar aapne sui ya safety pin dali, toh wo parda phat jayega aur phone hamesha ke liye non-waterproof ho jayega.

## 👂 Earpiece Speaker (Calling Speaker) Se Paani Kaise Nikalein?

Aksar log bottom main speaker toh saaf kar lete hain, lekin phone ke top par jo calling earpiece hota hai uski aawaz dabi reh jati hai:
1. Earpiece speaker ka grill bohot chota aur bareek hota hai.
2. FixMySpeakers ya YouTube par **"Ear Speaker Water Eject 300Hz"** sound search karein.
3. Call volume ko maximum 100% karein aur earpiece ko niche ki taraf jhuka kar rakhein.
4. Earpiece speaker high frequencies par vibrate karta hai, isliye 300Hz se 500Hz ki tone chalaane par chote droplets instantly nikal jate hain.

## 🎤 Agar Microphone (Mic) Mein Paani Chala Jaye?

Agar call par samne wale ko aapki aawaz bohot door se ya dabi hui sunayi de rahi hai, toh iska matlab primary microphone hole mein paani phasa hai:
- Bottom mein Type-C port ke bagal mein jo chota sa pin-hole hota hai wahi microphone hai.
- Is pin-hole mein sui ya pin daalne ki galti **kabhi na karein**, kyunki iske theek pichhe delicate mic diaphragm hota hai jo puncture ho sakta hai.
- Ek dry cotton bud (Q-tip) ke aage ka cotton halka sa patla karke mic hole ke muh par 10 second touch karke rakhein — cotton capillary action se sara paani kheench lega.

## 🔴 Liquid Contact Indicator (LDI) Check Kaise Karein?

Service center jane se pehle apna water damage status janne ke liye:
- Apne phone ka SIM card tray nikal kar bahar karein.
- Ek torch ya flashlight se SIM slot ke andar jhaankiye.
- Wahan ek chota sa square sticker hota hai jise **LDI (Liquid Damage Indicator)** kehte hain:
  - Agar sticker **White (Safed)** hai: Toh internal motherboard safe hai aur warranty valid hai.
  - Agar sticker **Pink ya Red (Laal)** ho chuka hai: Toh paani phone ke critical circuits tak pahunch chuka hai.
