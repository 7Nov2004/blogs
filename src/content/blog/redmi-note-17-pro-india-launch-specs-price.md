## Redmi Note 17 Pro Ke Official Specs

### Display
- **Size:** 6.83-inch 1.5K Flat AMOLED
- **Refresh Rate:** 120Hz adaptive
- **Brightness:** 3,500 nits peak brightness (sunlight mein bhi clearly visible)
- **Protection:** Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2

### Processor
- **Chipset:** Qualcomm Snapdragon 6s Gen 4
- Efficient performance with good gaming capability
- 5G support with all Indian bands

### Battery — Sabse Badi Khaasiyat ⚡
- **10,000mAh battery** — India mein pehla phone is segment mein itni badi battery ke saath
- Fast charging support
- **2 din+ battery backup** normal use mein (company claim)
- Aap phone mein kabhi "low battery anxiety" feel nahi karoge

### Camera
- **Main Camera:** 50MP primary sensor
- **Secondary Camera:** Ultra-wide lens
- **Front Camera:** 16MP selfie camera
- Night mode aur AI scene detection

### RAM & Storage
| Variant | RAM | Storage |
|---------|-----|---------|
| Base | 6GB | 128GB |
| Mid | 8GB | 256GB |
| Top | 12GB | 512GB |

### Durability — Is Segment Ka Champion 🏆
Yahan Redmi Note 17 Pro sabse alag hai:
- **IP68** — 1.5 meter paani mein 30 minutes
- **IP69** — High pressure water jets se bhi safe
- **IP69K** — Steam cleaning bhi survive kar sakta hai
- **TUV SUD Titan Certified** — 3 meter se girne par bhi protected
