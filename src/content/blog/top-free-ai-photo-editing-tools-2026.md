## 1. Clipdrop — The Swiss Army Knife of AI Photo Editing

**Clipdrop** ko Stability AI ke researchers ne develop kiya hai, aur ye single platform par 10 alag-alag AI photo editing powers deta hai.

### Killer Features:
- **Cleanup (Object Removal):** Photo mein peeche se koi anjaan tourist nikal raha hai ya electric wire aa rahi hai? Bas brush se use highlight karein, AI surrounding texture ko match karke use gayab kar dega.
- **Relight (Virtual Studio Lights):** Agar aapki selfie mein light kam hai, toh aap photo ke upar virtual 3D colored lights (neon blue, warm orange) place kar sakte hain jo bilkul real shadow create karti hain.
- **Uncrop (Image Extender):** Agar aapki photo square hai aur aapko use YouTube banner ya 16:9 wallpaper banana hai, toh ye AI photo ke charo taraf natural landscape khud draw kar deta hai!
- **Remove Background:** Hair strands aur difficult edges ko pixel-perfect cut karta hai.

## 2. Photopea — Browser Mein Chalne Wala Free Adobe Photoshop

Agar aapko layers, masks aur selection tools ka full professional control chahiye lekin aap Adobe Photoshop ka ₹2,500/month subscription afford nahi kar sakte, toh **Photopea** aapke liye vardaan hai.

### Why Photopea is Incredible:
- **Zero Software Installation:** Browser mein `photopea.com` kholein — 5 seconds mein poora Photoshop interface samne aa jata hai.
- **Supports PSD Files:** Photoshop ki .PSD, Illustrator ki .AI, aur Figma files direct open aur edit hoti hain.
- **AI Generative Fill Integration:** Selection lasso tool se area select karein aur prompt likhein — background automatically generate ho jayega!
- **100% Free:** Ye tool ads ke zariye chalta hai, isme koi hidden watermark ya paid paywall nahi hai.

Agar aap Amazon, Meesho, Flipkart ya Instagram shop par kapde, shoes, cosmetics ya electronics bechte hain, toh aapko mehenge camera photoshoot ki zaroorat nahi hai.

### How It Works:
