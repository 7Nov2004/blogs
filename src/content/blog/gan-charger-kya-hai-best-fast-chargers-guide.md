## 📊 Technical Comparison: Silicon vs Gallium Nitride (GaN) Chargers

| Feature | Traditional Silicon (Si) Charger | Modern GaN (Gallium Nitride) Charger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Semiconductor Material** | Silicon Crystal | Gallium Nitride (GaN Systems / Navitas) |
| **Bandgap Energy** | 1.1 eV (Lower Energy Efficiency) | 3.4 eV (Wide Bandgap - Higher Efficiency) |
| **Physical Size & Weight** | Heavy & Large Form Factor | 40% - 50% More Compact & Portable |
| **Heat Dissipation** | Heats up quickly during high wattage | Operates at much lower temperatures |
| **Charging Standards** | Basic QC / Standard PD | USB-PD 3.1, PPS, QC 4.0+, Apple Fast Charge |
| **Multi-Device Output** | Usually Single Port | Multi-Port (2x Type-C + 1x USB-A Dynamic Power Split) |

## ⚡ GaN Technology Kaise Kaam Karti Hai?

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["Wide Bandgap Semiconductor (GaN)"] --> B["Higher Switching Frequency (3x Fast)"]
    B --> C["Smaller Transformer & Components Needed"]
    C --> D["40% Smaller Size + Lower Thermal Heat"]
```

Traditional chargers mein internal electrical components ko insulate karne ke liye bade transformers aur heat sinks ki zaroorat hoti thi.

GaN (Gallium Nitride) ek aisa crystal material hai jo **higher electron mobility** deliver karta hai. Iska matlab hai ki internal switches 3x-5x fast speed par turn ON/OFF ho sakte hain. Switch frequency fast hone ki wajah se charger ke andar ke transformers aur capacitors boht chhote size ke lagaye ja sakte hain.

## 💡 Buyer Guide: GaN Charger Kharidte Waqt Ye 4 Baatein Check Karein

1. **PPS (Programmable Power Supply) Support:** Samsung Galaxy phones aur Pixel phones par super fast charging 2.0 ke liye charger mein **PPS Protocol** hona compulsory hai.
2. **Single Port Max Wattage:** Multi-port charger par likha 65W total wattage hota hai. Check karein ki **Single USB-C Port se minimum 65W output** milta hai ya nahi (Laptop charge karne ke liye 65W single port zaroori hai).
3. **Foldable Plug Design:** Travel utility ke liye foldable wall pins better rehti hain.
4. **BIS Certification:** Always check **BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) Safety Stamp** taaki Indian power fluctuations se protection mile.
