Kahi bahar jate waqt physical wallet mein Driving Licence (DL), car ya bike ki RC (Registration Certificate), insurance copy aur PAN card lekar ghoomna kaafi risk bhara hota hai. Agar wallet kho jaye ya barish mein bheeg jaye, toh original sarkari kagaz dubara banwane mein hafton ka samay aur RTO ke chakkar lagte hain. Lekin kya aap jaante hain ki Bharat Sarkar ka ek aisa official mobile app hai jisme aapke sabhi sarkari documents legally verify hokar save rehte hain aur Traffic Police ya Airport security par physical hard copy dikhane ki koi zaroorat nahi hoti? Is platform ka naam hai **DigiLocker**.

Is comprehensive Hindi guide mein hum dekhenge ki DigiLocker par account kaise banayein aur Driving Licence, Vehicle RC, PAN card aur Class 10th/12th marksheet kaise download karein.

## DigiLocker Kya Hai aur Iski Legal Validity Kya Hai?

DigiLocker Bharat Sarkar ke **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)** dwara shuru kiya gaya ek secure cloud-based document locker system hai. 

Information Technology (Preservation and Retention of Information by Intermediaries Providing Digital Locker Facilities) Rules, 2016 ke **Rule 9A** ke mutabiq:

```text
DigiLocker Issued Document  ===  Physical Original Government Document
(IT Act 2000 ke tehat barabar legal maanyata prapt)
```

Yani agar traffic police checking ke samay aapke paas physical licence ya RC nahi hai aur aap DigiLocker app mein digitally issued QR-verified document dikha dete hain, toh challan nahi katega. Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) ne sabhi state police departments ko iske baare mein circular jari kiya hua hai.

## DigiLocker Par Account Kaise Banayein (Step by Step)

DigiLocker app Android (Google Play Store) aur iPhone (Apple App Store) dono par available hai. Aap chaho toh computer par web browser se digilocker.gov.in par bhi ja sakte hain:

### Step 1: App Install Karein aur Registration Shuru Karein
1. Play Store ya App Store se official **DigiLocker** app install karein.
2. Apni bhasha (Hindi ya English) select karein aur **"Get Started"** par tap karein.
3. Ab **"Create Account"** button par click karein.

### Step 2: Basic Details Enter Karein
Screen par aane wale form mein wahi details bharein jo aapke Aadhaar Card par likhi hain:
- **Full Name** (Aadhaar ke mutabiq)
- **Date of Birth (DOB)**
- **Gender** (Male / Female / Other)
- **Mobile Number**
- **Set 6-digit Security PIN** (Ye PIN login karte waqt har baar kaam aayega)
- **Email ID** aur **Aadhaar Number**

### Step 3: OTP Verification
- Aapke Aadhaar registered mobile number par ek 6-digit OTP aayega. OTP daal kar **Submit** par tap karein.
- Ab aapka DigiLocker account successfully create ho chuka hai!

## 1. Driving Licence (DL) DigiLocker Mein Kaise Download Karein

Car ya bike chalate waqt physical DL bhool jane par DigiLocker ka digital licence sabse bada savior banta hai:

```text
DigiLocker App Open Karein  ──►  Search Tab Mein Type Karein "Driving Licence"
                                              ↓
                        Ministry of Road Transport (MoRTH) Select Karein
                                              ↓
                           Apna DL Number aur DOB Enter Karein
                                              ↓
                                   "Get Document" Par Tap Karein
```

Kuch hi seconds mein MoRTH ke Sarathi database se aapka official DL pull ho jayega aur **"Issued Documents"** tab mein save ho jayega. Is document par ek unique QR Code aur Ashoka Pillar emblem hota hai jise traffic police machine se scan karke on-the-spot verify kar sakti hai.

## 2. Bike ya Car Ki RC (Vehicle Registration) Kaise Add Karein

Aapke naam par register kisi bhi vehicle ki RC nikalna behad aasan hai:

1. DigiLocker app ke home page par **Search** icon par tap karein.
2. **"Registration of Vehicles"** likh kar search karein aur *Ministry of Road Transport and Highways* select karein.
3. Ab do details fill karein:
   - **Registration Number:** Gadi ka number (e.g. DL01AB1234)
   - **Chassis Number:** Gadi ke chassis number ke aakhiri 5 digits.
4. **"Get Document"** button dabayein.
5. Aapki digital RC turant generate hokar offline access ke liye save ho jayegi.

## 3. Class 10th aur 12th Ki Marksheet Kaise Fetch Karein

Chahe aapne CBSE, ICSE ya kisi bhi State Board (UP Board, Bihar Board, Maharashtra Board, etc.) se padhai ki ho, college admission aur job interviews ke liye marksheet nikalna ab second-bhar ka kaam hai:

1. Search tab mein jakar apne education board ka naam search karein (jaise *CBSE* ya *UP State Board*).
2. **Class X Marksheet** ya **Class XII Marksheet** option par tap karein.
3. Apna **Passing Year** aur **Roll Number** enter karein.
4. **Get Document** par click karein. Board ke server se digitally signed marksheet PDF aapke locker mein aa jayegi.

## Issued Documents vs Uploaded Drive: Farak Samjhein

Kayi log DigiLocker mein apne phone ki gallery se photo ya PDF upload kar dete hain aur sochte hain ki wo legal hai. Aisa bilkul nahi hai:

| Feature | Issued Documents (Official) | DigiLocker Drive (Uploaded Files) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Source** | Govt database se direct fetch | Phone gallery/scanner se user dwara upload |
| **Legal Validity** | Original physical document ke barabar legal | Sirf personal backup, legal proof nahi |
| **QR Code Verification** | Official cryptographic digital signature | Normal scanned PDF / image |
| **Traffic Challan Exemption** | Challan maaf hota hai | Police ise accept karne se mana kar sakti hai |
| **Storage Limit** | Unlimited government certificates | 1 GB Free Cloud Storage |

## DigiLocker Use Karte Waqt 3 Important Safety Tips

1. **6-Digit PIN Kisi Se Share Na Karein:** DigiLocker mein aapke sabhi sensitive documents hote hain. Kabhi bhi kisi call ya SMS par apna PIN ya Aadhaar OTP na batayein taaki aap [Digital Arrest Scam](/blog/digital-arrest-scam-kya-hai-kaise-bachein-hindi/) aur identity theft se surakshit rahein.
2. **Offline Download Save Rakhein:** Kahi safar par nikalne se pehle Issued tab mein jakar documents ko ek baar view kar lein taaki agar jungle ya highway par internet network slow ho, tab bhi offline document open ho sake.
3. **Family Members Ke Documents:** DigiLocker Aadhaar-linked individual account hota hai. Isliye har family member ka alag DigiLocker account hona chahiye.
