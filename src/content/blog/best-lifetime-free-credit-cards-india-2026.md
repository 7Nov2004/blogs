1. **Sale Ke Dauran Instant Discounts:** Amazon Great Indian Festival ya Flipkart Big Billion Days ke dauran ICICI aur HDFC cards par flat 10% instant discount milta hai. 2. **Auto-Debit Setup Rakhein:** Chahe card free ho, bill time par pay na karne par late fine sabhi par lagta hai. Hamesha bill payment ke liye auto-debit on rakhein.
