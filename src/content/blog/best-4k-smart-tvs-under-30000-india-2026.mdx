## 💡 Smart TV Kharidne Se Pehle Yeh 4 Baatein Zaroor Check Karein:

1. **RAM Kam Se Kam 2GB Ho:** Kabhi bhi 1GB ya 1.5GB RAM wala Smart TV na lein, woh 6 mahine baad hang hone lagta hai.
2. **Dual-Band Wi-Fi Support:** 4K YouTube aur Netflix stream karne ke liye 5GHz Wi-Fi zaroori hota hai.
3. **HDMI 2.1 Ports:** Agar aap PS5 ya gaming console connect karna chahte hain, toh minimum 3 HDMI ports hone chahiye.
4. **Wall Mount Installation:** Brand ke official technician se hi installation karwayein taaki warranty claim mein koi issue na aaye.
