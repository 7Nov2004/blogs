Imagine kijiye: Aap shaam ko ghar par baithe hain aur achanak aapke phone ka network gayab ho jata hai. Screen par likha aata hai: **"No Service / SIM Inactive"**.

Aap sochte hain ki shayad tower mein koi temporary technical issue hoga aur aap so jaate hain.

Agli subah jab aap uthte hain aur Wi-Fi connect karke email check karte hain, toh aapke hosh udd jaate hain — **Aapke bank account se 5 lakh rupaye alag-alag transactions ke zariye nikaal liye gaye hain!**

Aur sabse hairani ki baat: **Aapke paas koi bank OTP nahi aaya, aapne koi link click nahi kiya, aur aapne kisi ko koi password nahi bataya!**

Ye koi fictional kahani nahi hai; ye hai Bharat ka sabse khatarnak silent financial crime — **SIM Swap Fraud (सिम स्वैप फ्रॉड)**.

Is comprehensive awareness guide mein hum dekhenge ki SIM Swap fraud kaise kaam karta hai, hackers bina OTP ke aapka bank account kaise loot lete hain, aur aap apne phone ko isse 100% surakshit kaise rakh sakte hain!

## SIM Swap Scam Kaam Kaise Karta Hai? (Step-by-Step Breakdown)

Hackers is scam ko 4 stages mein anjaam dete hain:

```
Stage 1: Aapka Personal Data Collect Karna (Phishing / Dark Web)
   ⬇
Stage 2: Fake Customer Care Call ("5G Upgrade / KYC Update")
   ⬇
Stage 3: Duplicate SIM Card Issue Karwana (Aapka SIM Band)
   ⬇
Stage 4: Net Banking Hack & Total Financial Drain
```

---

### Step 1: Information Gathering
Hackers pehle se aapke baare mein basic jankari ikattha kar lete hain (Aapka naam, Date of Birth, Aadhaar number aur registered mobile number). Ye data aam tor par online data breaches, fake job portals ya phishing messages se chori hota hai.

### Step 2: The Social Engineering Hook
Scammer aapko call karta hai aur khud ko Jio ya Airtel ka senior executive batata hai:
> *"Sir, aapka 4G SIM kal se band ho jayega. Ise 5G mein upgrade karne ke liye ya e-SIM activate karne ke liye humne aapko ek SMS bheja hai, usme likha 19-digit number ya QR code hume forward karein."*

### Step 3: Duplicate SIM Activation
Jaise hi hacker ko confirmation milti hai ya wo fake forged ID card lekar kisi local telecom retailer ke paas jata hai, wo kehta hai: *"Mera phone chori ho gaya tha, mujhe is number ka duplicate SIM chahiye."*

Retailer duplicate SIM issue kar deta hai.
- **Result:** **Hacker ka duplicate SIM chalu ho jata hai, aur aapka asli physical SIM instant DEAD (No Service) ho jata hai!**

### Step 4: The 2-Hour Dark Window
Ab hacker ke paas aapka zinda phone number hai!
1. Wo aapki Net Banking website par jata hai.
2. Click karta hai: **"Forgot Password / Reset Login"**.
3. Bank verification OTP bhejta hai — **aur OTP seedha hacker ke phone par chala jata hai!**
4. Hacker naya password banata hai, naye beneficiaries add karta hai aur RTGS/IMPS ke zariye poora account khali kar deta hai.

## 3 Warning Signs: Kaise Pata Karein Ki Aapka SIM Swap Ho Raha Hai?

1. **Achanak "No Service" Aana:** Agar aapke ghar ya office mein sabka mobile network chal raha hai aur sirf aapka SIM inactive ho gaya hai aur restart karne par bhi network nahi aa raha.
2. **Strange Calls / Harassment:** Fraud karne se thodi der pehle scammers lagatar aapko spam calls karte hain taaki aap pareshan hokar apna phone Switch Off kar dein aur unhe duplicate SIM activate karne ka time mil jaye.
3. **Unexpected SMS from Operator:** Telecom company se bina aapke request kiye message aana: *"Your SIM change request has been initiated."*

## SIM Swap Se Bachne Ke 5 Must-Do Security Steps

### 1. SIM Card Lock (SIM PIN) Enable Karein
Ye setting aapke phone ko impenetrable bana deti hai:
- Android: Settings ➔ **Security & Privacy** ➔ **More Security Settings** ➔ **SIM Card Lock** ko ON karein.
- iPhone: Settings ➔ **Mobile Data** ➔ **SIM PIN** ko ON karein.
- Ek 4-digit PIN set karein (Default PIN 1234 ya 0000 hota hai).
- Iske baad jab bhi koi SIM ko kisi doosre phone mein dalega, bina PIN ke SIM chal hi nahi sakta!

### 2. TAFCOP Portal Par Check Karein Kitne SIM Chal Rahe Hain
Government of India ke official portal par abhi check karein:
1. Browser mein kholein: `tafcop.sancharsaathi.gov.in`
2. Apna mobile number aur OTP enter karein.
3. Screen par un sabhi phone numbers ki list aa jayegi jo aapke Aadhaar card par registered hain.
4. Agar koi aisa number dikhe jo aapne nahi khareeda, toh turant **"Not Required"** ya **"Not My Number"** par click karke block karwa dein.

### 3. SMS OTP Ke Bajaye Authenticator App Use Karein
SMS OTP 2026 mein sabse weak security link hai.
- Apne Google account, Instagram, WhatsApp aur financial apps par **Google Authenticator** ya **Microsoft Authenticator** on karein.
- Authenticator app internet ya mobile network par depend nahi karti, isiliye SIM Swap hone par bhi hacker aapka account login nahi kar sakta.

### 4. e-SIM QR Code Kabhi Kisi Ko Forward Mat Karein
Telecom companies kabhi bhi email ya WhatsApp par e-SIM QR code scan karne ko nahi bolti. Agar kisi ne aapka e-SIM apne phone par download kar liya toh aapka network instantly chala jayega.

## Agar Aapka SIM Achanak Band Ho Jaye Toh Turant Kya Karein?

Bina ek second gawaaye ye emergency action lein:

1. **Ghar Ke Kisi Doosre Phone Se Customer Care Call Karein:** Jio (198), Airtel (121), Vi (199) par call karein aur customer care se puchein ki aapka SIM kyun band hua hai. Agar unhone kaha ki SIM swap request aayi hai, toh turant use **EMERGENCY BLOCK** karwayein!
2. **Apne Bank Ko Call Karke Net Banking & UPI Freeze Karwayein:** Bank ke customer care par emergency debit card/netbanking block karwayein.
3. **Dial 1930 (National Cyber Helpline):** Agar koi transaction ho gaya hai toh 1930 par call karein taaki government account freeze kar sake.
