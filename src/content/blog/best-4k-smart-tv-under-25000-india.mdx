## 4K Smart TV Kharidte Waqt Ye 4 Baatein Zaroor Check Karein

### 1. 4K UHD (3840x2160) vs Full HD (1080p)
43-inch display size par 1080p display lena sabse badi galti hoti hai kyunki screen badi hone par pixels alag-alag dikhne lagte hain. Hamesha confirm karein ki TV **True 4K (Ultra HD)** ho.

### 2. Ram & Storage (Minimum 2GB RAM)
Saste TVs mein companies 1GB RAM dekar bachte hain jisse 3 mahine baad TV ka remote hang hone lagta hai aur apps crash karti hain. Hamesha **minimum 2GB RAM aur 8GB/16GB Storage** wala TV hi chunein.

### 3. HDMI Ports (Minimum 3 Ports with eARC)
Check karein ki TV mein minimum 3 HDMI ports hon taaki Setup Box, Gaming Console aur Soundbar ek sath connect reh sakein bina cable nikaale.

### 4. High-Speed Internet Connection
4K streaming smoothly chalane ke liye minimum 30-50 Mbps internet speed chahiye hoti hai. Broadband selection ke liye hamara [Jio AirFiber vs Airtel Xstream Fiber 2026 Comparison](/blog/jio-airfiber-vs-airtel-xstream-hindi-2026) zaroor padhein!
