## Smartwatch Kharidte Samay In 5 Technical Baaton Ka Dhyan Rakhein

### 1. AMOLED vs TFT / LCD Panel
₹3,000 se upar ke budget mein TFT ya LCD display wali watch **kabhi mat khareedein**. AMOLED screens mein true black contrast hota hai, dhoop mein visibility behtar hoti hai, aur Always-On Display (AOD) feature sirf AMOLED par hi power-efficient rehta hai.

### 2. IP68 Water Resistance
Gym workout ke paseene aur barsaat se bachne ke liye smartwatch mein minimum **IP68 rating** hona compulsory hai taaki hand wash karte waqt ya baarish mein watch kharab na ho.

### 3. App Experience & Data Privacy
Smartwatch utni hi achi hoti hai jitni achi uski companion mobile app ho. Amazfit (Zepp app), OnePlus (N Health), aur Noise ki companion apps data sync fast karti hain aur background phone battery kam khati hain.

### 4. Bluetooth Calling (Single Chip vs Dual Chip)
Purani watches mein calling ke liye 2 alag Bluetooth connections banane padte the jo phone ki battery khatam kar dete the. Hamesha **Single Chip BT Calling (Bluetooth 5.3)** wali watch chunein.
