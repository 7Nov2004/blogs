Aaj ke digital daur mein PAN card aur Aadhaar card dono hi sabse zaroori identity aur financial documents ban chuke hain. Income Tax Department aur Government of India ne sabhi citizens ke liye PAN ko Aadhaar se jodna anivarya (mandatory) kar diya hai. Bahut se logon ko lagta hai ki unka card pehle se jud chuka hai, lekin kai bar technical error, name mismatch ya pending verification ki wajah se status 'Inoperative' reh jata hai. Agar aapka PAN card deactivate ho gaya toh bank transactions, salary credit, aur mutual fund SIP tak ruk sakti hain. Is comprehensive guide mein hum detail mein dekhenge ki 2026 mein bina kisi agent ke ghar baithe sirf 2 minute mein PAN Aadhaar link status kaise check karein.

## PAN Aadhaar Link Status Check Karna Kyun Zaroori Hai?

Agar aapne abhi tak apna status verify nahi kiya hai, toh ye samajhna zaroori hai ki iska asar aapke rozmarra ke financial kaamon par kaise padta hai:

Pehli baat, jab PAN card inoperative ho jata hai toh Income Tax Act ke rules ke mutabiq aap kisi bhi financial year ka ITR file nahi kar pate aur na hi pending tax refund claim kar pate hain. Dusri baat, bank account mein high-value transactions par TDS normal rate se double yaani 20% tak kat sakta hai. Teesri badi pareshani ye aati hai ki stock market trading, demat account, aur [SIP mutual fund investment](/blog/sip-mutual-fund-kaise-shuroo-karein-2026-hindi-guide/) ke KYC records hold par chale jaate hain.

Isliye har individual ko samay-samay par apna status confirm kar lena chahiye taaki aage chalkar kisi emergency mein financial block ka samna na karna pade. [Aadhaar card address update process](/blog/aadhaar-card-address-change-online-2026-hindi-guide/) ki tarah ye verification bhi poori tarah digital hai.

## Method 1 — Income Tax e-Filing Portal Se Status Check (Sabse Fast)

Income Tax Department ka official portal `incometax.gov.in` sabse reliable aur instant rasta hai. Isme aapko koi account create karne ya password yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi padti:

**Step 1: Official Portal Open Karein**  
Sabse pehle apne smartphone ya computer ke browser mein official address open karein: `www.incometax.gov.in`. Hamesha dhyan rakhein ki kisi fake ya third-party phishing link par click na karein.

**Step 2: 'Link Aadhaar Status' Option Par Jayein**  
Homepage par left-hand side par aapko 'Quick Links' ka section dikhai dega. Wahan dusre ya teesre number par **Link Aadhaar Status** ka direct option milega. Us par tap karein.

**Step 3: PAN aur Aadhaar Details Enter Karein**  
Ab aapke samne ek simple form open hoga jisme 2 fields hongi:
- **PAN:** Apna 10-digit alphanumeric PAN card number likhein (Jaise: `ABCDE1234F`).
- **Aadhaar Number:** Apna 12-digit UIDAI Aadhaar number enter karein.

**Step 4: 'View Link Aadhaar Status' Par Click Karein**  
Details fill karne ke baad neeche diye gaye blue button **View Link Aadhaar Status** par click karein. Screen par turant pop-up notification aa jayega jo aapka exact current status show karega.

## Screen Par Aane Wale Status Messages Ka Matlab

Jab aap status check karte hain, toh mukhya roop se teen alag-alag messages dikh sakte hain:

1. **"Your PAN is already linked to given Aadhaar"**  
Iska matlab aapko bilkul chinta karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Aapka record Income Tax database aur UIDAI dono taraf se 100% verified aur linked hai.

2. **"PAN not linked with Aadhaar. Please click on Link Aadhaar to link your Aadhaar with PAN"**  
Iska matlab aapka PAN abhi tak jodna baaki hai. Agar date nikal chuki hai toh aapko Rs. 1,000 late fee challan pay karke turant link request daalni hogi.

3. **"Aadhaar-PAN linking request is pending with NSDL / UTIITSL for validation"**  
Iska matlab aapne request submit kar di hai aur department level par matching aur verification process progress mein hai. 3 se 5 din mein ye clear ho jayega.

## Method 2 — SMS Ke Zariye Status Kaise Check Karein

Agar aapke area mein internet connection slow hai ya aap smartphone use nahi kar rahe hain, toh basic SMS facility se bhi status verify ho jata hai:

Aapko apne mobile ke messaging app mein jakar ye format type karna hai:

```text
UIDPAN <12 Digit Aadhaar Number> <10 Digit PAN Number>
```

**Example Format:**
```text
UIDPAN 123456789012 ABCDE1234F
```

Is message ko **567678** ya **56161** par send kar dijiye. Kuch hi der mein Income Tax Department ki taraf se ek reply SMS aayega jisme bataya jayega ki dono documents linked hain ya nahi. Note karein ki SMS usi mobile number se bhejna behtar hota hai jo documents ke saath registered ho.

## Method 3 — e-Filing Account Login Karke Profile Section Mein Check Karna

Agar aap regularly ITR bharte hain aur aapka account pehle se bana hua hai:

1. Portal par Login button par click karein.
2. User ID (aapka PAN) aur password dalkar dashboard par jayein.
3. Top-right corner par apni Profile par tap karein.
4. Profile Details mein 'Aadhaar' section dekhein.
5. Wahan green tick ke saath "Linked" status clearly mention hota hai.

Agar wahan unlinked show ho raha hai toh dashboard se hi direct Link Aadhaar tab par jakar verification initiate kiya ja sakta hai. Jaise [Voter ID Aadhaar linking portal](/blog/voter-id-aadhaar-link-kaise-kare-online-2026/) par records match hote hain, wahi mechanism yahan kaam karta hai.

## Name Mismatch Ya Rejection Ki Samasya Kaise Theek Karein?

Bahut se logon ka status link hone ke bajay reject ho jata hai. Iska sabse bada reason hota hai **Demographic Mismatch**. Yaani aapke PAN card par naam ki spelling, Date of Birth ya Father's Name aapke Aadhaar data se match nahi kar raha hota:

Agar Aadhaar card par naam "Ramesh Kumar Verma" hai aur PAN card par sirf "Ramesh Verma", toh automated biometric matching system verification ko deny kar deta hai. Aise mamle mein:
- Pehle check karein ki kaunsa document sahi hai.
- Agar PAN card galat hai, toh NSDL portal par jakar online PAN Correction Form bharein.
- Agar Aadhaar galat hai, toh UIDAI myAadhaar portal se online correction apply karein.
- Dono records identical hone ke baad hi linking process initiate karein.

## PAN Inoperative Hone Par Kya Karein?

Agar status check karne par pata chalta hai ki aapka PAN pehle hi inoperative ho chuka hai, toh ghabraane ki zaroorat nahi hai:

Aapko e-filing portal par jakar NSDL e-Gov (Protean) ke zariye Minor Head 500 (Fee) ke tehat **Rs. 1,000 ka Challan** bharna hoga. Payment complete hone ke baad portal par jakar dobara 'Link Aadhaar' section mein details submit karein. Tax department typically 48 se 72 hours ke andar status ko wapas 'Operative' mark kar deta hai.

Apna PAN Aadhaar link status check karna behad aasan aur muft hai, jisme 2 minute se zyada ka waqt nahi lagta. Financial safety aur smooth banking operations ke liye aaj hi upar bataye gaye steps ko follow karke apna status confirm karein. Agar link nahi hai, toh bina deri kiye request submit karein taaki kisi achanak aane wale bank freeze ya heavy tax deduction se bacha ja sake.
