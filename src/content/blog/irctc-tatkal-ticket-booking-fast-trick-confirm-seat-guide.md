| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AC Classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC)** | Subah theek **10:00 AM** | 10:15 AM ke baad | **Zero Refund** (0% refund after confirmation) |

## Trick #1: Booking Se 24 Ghante Pehle "Master List" Setup Karein

Tatkal booking fail hone ka sabse bada kaaran hota hai passenger details (Name, Age, Berths) manual type karna. IRCTC ne khud iska official solution diya hai jise bohot log ignore karte hain:

### Master List Kaise Banayein (Step-by-Step):
1. Tatkal khulne se kam se kam 2 ghante pehle (ya pichli raat) IRCTC App ya Website par login karein.
2. **My Account** section mein jayein aur **"My Master List"** option par click karein.
3. **Add Passenger** par click karein aur un sabhi logon ki details daalein jinki ticket book karni hai:
   - Full Name (Jo Aadhar card par hai).
   - Accurate Date of Birth aur Gender.
   - Berth Preference (Hamesha **"No Preference"** chunein — isse allocation tezi se hota hai).
   - Food Choice (Agar train mein catering applicable ho).
4. Save kar dein. Ab ye passengers aapki profile mein permanently link ho chuke hain.

**Booking ke waqt:** Jab Tatkal time par aap train select karenge, toh "Add Passenger" par click karne ke bajaye seedha **"Select from Master List"** par tick karein — 2 second mein sabhi yatri select ho jayenge!

## 🛡️ Vikalp Scheme: Agar Tatkal Miss Ho Jaye Toh Backup

3. Agar aapki primary train mein seat confirm nahi hui, toh Railway system bina kisi extra charge ke aapko doosri train ki vacant seat par automatically confirm ticket allot kar deta hai!

## 5 Galtiyan Jo Tatkal Me Kabhi Mat Karna

1. **Page Refresh (F5) Mat Dabana:** Jab payment ho rahi ho ya loading icon ghoom raha ho, toh ghabra kar browser refresh na karein. IRCTC session kill ho jayega.
2. **Multiple Devices Se Ek Hi Account Login Na Karein:** Ek hi IRCTC ID ko phone app aur laptop dono par ek sath login karne par security bot suspicious activity samajh kar account 1 ghante ke liye block kar deta hai.
3. **Invalid Captcha Entry:** Captcha ko hamesha dhyan se padhein. 2026 mein IRCTC ne audio captcha aur math puzzles bhi diye hain jo fast type ho jaate hain.
4. **Third-Party Auto-Booker Bots Se Bachein:** Illegal extensions ya paid Telegram bots use karne se bachein. Railway Protection Force (RPF) aise IP addresses ko blacklist kar deti hai aur Cyber Act ke tahat penalty lagti hai.
5. **Slow Wi-Fi Connection:** Tatkal ke dauran 5G mobile data ya high-speed fiber broadband ka use karein taaki packet loss zero rahe.
