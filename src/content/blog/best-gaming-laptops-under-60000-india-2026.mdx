## Gaming Laptop Kharidte Waqt Ye 4 Baatein Zaroor Check Karein

### 1. 6GB VRAM vs 4GB VRAM
Pehle ke saste RTX 3050 models 4GB VRAM ke sath aate the. 2026 mein **sirf 6GB VRAM** wala model hi lein. 4GB VRAM naye AAA games mein insufficient padne laga hai.

### 2. TGP Wattage Check
Dukan wale ke kehne par mat jaiye. Manufacturer specification sheet mein dekhein ki TGP **minimum 75W se 95W** ke beech ho. 45W TGP wala laptop lene par 30% FPS ka nuksaan hoga.

### 3. Dual-Channel 16GB RAM
Gaming laptops mein hamesha 2 RAM sticks (`8GB x 2`) hona zaroori hai. Dual-channel memory se CPU ka gaming communication 15% fast ho jata hai.

### 4. Cooling & Laptop Stand
Gaming laptop ko kabhi bed ya rajai par rakh kar mat chalayein kyunki niche ke intake vents block ho jaate hain. Hamesha table par ya ek basic laptop stand ke sath use karein.
