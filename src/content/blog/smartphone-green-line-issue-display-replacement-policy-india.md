## Technical Root Cause: Green Line Aakhir Aati Kyu Hai?

Bohot se log sochte hain ki ye ek software bug hai jise naye update se theek kiya ja sakta hai. Lekin technical sachai ye hai ki ye ek **Hardware Failure** hai jo software update ke dauran trigger hota hai:

### 1. Chip-on-Plastic (COP) Ribbon Cable Degradation
Modern bezel-less AMOLED screens mein display controller chip ko screen ke bottom par ek ultra-thin flexible ribbon cable se bond kiya jata hai. Is bonding ko microscopic conductive adhesive se joda jata hai.

### 2. Update Ke Dauran High Thermal Stress
Jab phone ek bada OS update (jaise Android 15 ya 16) download aur unpack karta hai, toh CPU aur GPU 100% capacity par 20 se 30 minute tak lagatar run karte hain. Is dauran phone ka internal temperature 45°C se 50°C cross kar jata hai.

### 3. Display Driver IC (DDIC) Ka Heat Short-Circuit
Is extreme heat ke kaaran display panel ki flexible ribbon cable par laga microscopic adhesive pighal jata hai ya loose ho jata hai. Jaise hi display controller ka specific vertical data trace disconnect hota hai, AMOLED ke sub-pixels par full voltage pass ho jati hai — jisse screen par ek permanent bright green line ban jati hai.

## Service Center Jane Se Pehle Ye 4 Kaam Zaroor Karein

Free display replacement claim karne ke liye aapki preparation bohot zaroori hai, warna service centers aksar "Physical Impact" ka bahana banakar claim reject karne ki koshish karte hain:

### 1. 360-Degree Video aur High-Res Photos Click Karein
Doosre phone se apne device ki har angle se video banayein:
- Display par koi hairline scratch ya chip na dikhe.
- Chaaro corners par koi dent ya drop mark na ho.
- Screen on karke green line clear record karein.

### 2. Complete Data Backup Lein
Display badalne ke dauran service center wale policy ke mutabik aapka phone factory reset karte hain. Apne WhatsApp chats, photos aur contacts ka Google Drive backup zaroor lein. Storage optimize karne ke liye hamara [Phone Storage Full Hone Par Clean Karne Ka Guide](/blog/phone-storage-full-kaise-khali-kare-tips/) follow karein.

### 3. Liquid Damage Indicator (LDI) Check Karein
SIM tray nikal kar andar jhaankiye. Wahan ek chhota sa white sticker hota hai jise LDI bolte hain. Agar wo white hai toh safe hai; agar wo pink ya red ho chuka hai, toh service center bolega ki phone paani mein gaya tha aur claim reject ho sakta hai.

### 4. Job Sheet Par Wording Dhyan Se Padh Kar Sign Karein
Jab service center executive aapko Job Sheet deta hai, toh "Customer Remark" mein check karein ki wahan **"No External Physical Damage Found"** clearly likha ho. Agar wo "Scratches on body" likhein, toh objection karein.

## Step-by-Step: Free Replacement Claim Process

Agar executive free replacement dene se mana kare ya bole ki "Warranty khatam ho gayi hai", toh is professional approach ko follow karein:

1. **Brand Ki Official Notice Dikhayein:** OnePlus aur Samsung ne officially press releases aur community forums par green line circulars issue kiye hain. Executive ko brand guidelines cite karein.
2. **Manager Se Escalation:** Counter staff se behes karne ke bajaye Service Center Manager se politely request karein aur batayein ki ye update ke baad achanak hua hardware defect hai jo universally acknowledged hai.
3. **Twitter (X) Support Tag Karein:** Service center mein baithkar hi apne phone ki photo ke saath brand ke official handle (jaise `@OnePlus_IN` ya `@SamsungSupportIN`) ko tag karein. Social media escalations par brands 2 ghante ke andar priority ticket generate karte hain.
4. **National Consumer Helpline (NCH):** Agar koi brand bilkul cooperate na kare, toh Govt of India ke NCH portal (consumerhelpline.gov.in) ya Umang App par complaint lodge karein. Iska resolution rate 90%+ rehta hai.

## Future Mein Green Line Se Phone Ko Kaise Bachayein?

Future mein AMOLED display failure ke risk ko minimize karne ke liye ye best practices apnayein:

- **Update Karte Waqt Phone Cover Hata Dein:** Phone cover heat ko trap karta hai. OS update install karte waqt silicon ya hard case nikal dein.
- **Charging Ke Dauran Update Install Na Karein:** Charging aur CPU heavy load ek saath milkar phone ka temperature dangerous level par le jaate hain. Hamesha phone ko 80% charge karke, bina charger lagaye, thandi jagah (AC room ya fan ke samne) update karein. Agar charging slow chal rahi ho toh hamara [Fast Charging Problem Solution Guide](/blog/phone-fast-charging-nahi-ho-rahi-problem-solution/) padhein.
- **Heavy Gaming Ke Turant Baad Update Na Karein:** BGMI ya Genshin Impact khelne ke baad phone ko 15 minute normal temperature par aane dein, uske baad hi update trigger karein.
