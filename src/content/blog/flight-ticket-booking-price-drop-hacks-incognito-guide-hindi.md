## 🕵️ Incognito Mode Aur Cookies Ka Sach: Myth vs Reality

Internet par har YouTuber bolta hai: *"Incognito mode mein search karo, ticket aadhi keemat par milegi!"* Aaiye iski technical sachai samjhein:

| Factor | Regular Browser Window | Incognito Mode (Private) | Real Impact on Price |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tracking Cookies** | Store hoti hain (Site ko pata hota hai aap dubara aaye) | Session close hote hi delete ho jati hain | Retargeting ads aur fake urgency countdown band ho jata hai |
| **Airline Inventory Algorithm** | Real-time seat bucket check karta hai | Same seat bucket check karta hai | Base fare par sirf 5% se 10% ka asar padta hai |
| **IP Address Location** | ISP location visible hoti hai | ISP location visible hoti hai | Zero difference (Jab tak VPN use na ho) |
| **Verdict** | Retargeting se price badh sakta hai | Clean fresh session milta hai | ⭐ **Hamesha Incognito use karna safer hai** |

## ✈️ Trick #1: Google Flights Ka "Date Grid" & "Price Graph"

MakeMyTrip ya Goibibo par seedha search karne ke bajaye hamesha **Google Flights** (`google.com/travel/flights`) se shuruwat karein:

### Google Flights Ka Secret Weapon:
1. Apna Origin aur Destination select karein.
2. Date picker par click karne ke bajaye **"Date Grid"** aur **"Price Graph"** option par tap karein.
3. Yahan aapko ek glance mein dikhega ki agar aap Friday ke bajaye Thursday ko fly karein, toh ticket **₹2,000 sasti** mil rahi hai!
4. **Track Prices Toggle ON Karein:** "Track prices" switch ko enable kar dein. Jaise hi us route par airline price drop karegi, Google seedha aapke Gmail par notification bhej dega!

## ⏰ Trick #4: 24-Hour Free Cancellation Rule (DGCA Mandate)

Ye DGCA ka official consumer protection rule hai jise 95% passengers nahi jaante:

> **Rule:** Agar aapki flight departure date mein kam se kam **7 din ka time baaki hai**, toh ticket book karne ke **theek 24 ghante ke andar** aap ticket ko bilkul FREE mein cancel kar sakte hain — bina kisi airline penalty ya cancellation fee ke!

**Smart Use Case:** Agar aapko koi achha fare dikh raha hai lekin leaves approve hone mein thoda doubt hai, toh ticket book kar lijiye. Agar agle din plan change hota hai, toh 24 hours ke andar cancel karke poora refund le sakte hain.
