## 1. "Smart 5G / 5G Auto" Mode Ko On Karein

Bahut se log apne phone ke developer settings ya secret menu mein jakar forcefully **"5G Only" (NR Only)** lock kar dete hain. Ye sabse badi galti hai!

Jab aap sirf WhatsApp text message padh rahe hote hain ya email check karte hain, tab bhi aapka phone full-power 5G transmitters ko active rakhta hai.

### Sahi Setting:
- Settings ➔ **Mobile Network / SIM Cards** ➔ **SIM 1 / SIM 2** mein jayein.
- Preferred Network Type mein **"5G/4G/3G/2G (Auto)"** ya **"Smart 5G"** select karein.
- iPhone users: Settings ➔ Mobile Data ➔ Mobile Data Options ➔ Voice & Data mein jakar **"5G Auto"** select karein (5G On mat select karein).

---

## 2. Weak Signal Area Mein Network "Ping-Pong" Effect Se Bachein

Jab aap basement, underground metro stations, ya concrete buildings ke andar hote hain jahan 5G ka sirf 1 bar aa raha hota hai, toh phone lagatar 4G aur 5G ke beech switch karta rehta hai. Is process ko telecom engineering mein **"Network Hunting / Ping-Pong"** kehte hain.

Is dauran phone ka modem tower ko catch karne ke liye **maximum wattage transmit** karta hai jisse phone pocket mein aag ki tarah garam ho jata hai.

> **Golden Rule:** Agar kisi jagah 5G signal unstable hai, toh bas Quick Settings se kuch ghante ke liye Network ko **4G (LTE)** par switch kar lijiye. Aapki battery seedha 30% bachegi.

---

## 3. Dynamic / LTPO 120Hz Refresh Rate Setup

Naye phones mein 120Hz AMOLED display bohot smooth scrolling deta hai, lekin jab 5G aur 120Hz dono ek sath chalte hain, toh battery paani ki tarah behne lagti hai.

### Fix Kaise Karein:
- Settings ➔ **Display & Brightness** ➔ **Screen Refresh Rate** par jayein.
- Ise "Standard 60Hz" ya **"Auto-Select / Adaptive"** par set karein.
- Isse static page padhte waqt screen automatically 30Hz/60Hz par drop ho jayegi aur gaming ya fast scroll par 120Hz hogi.

---

## 4. Background Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Scanning Ko Disable Karein

Aapko lagta hai ki aapne Wi-Fi aur Bluetooth band kar diya hai toh wo band hai? Asal mein Android system location accuracy improve karne ke liye background mein lagatar aas-paas ke Wi-Fi routers ko scan karta rehta hai.

### Kaise Band Karein:
1. Settings ➔ **Location** ➔ **Location Services** (ya Scanning Settings) mein jayein.
2. **Wi-Fi Scanning** toggle ko **Turn OFF** karein.
3. **Bluetooth Scanning** toggle ko bhi **Turn OFF** karein.
4. Is single tweak se battery ka standby drain 50% kam ho jata hai.

---

## 5. Aggressive Background App Battery Management

Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, aur Telegram jaisi apps 5G connection ka istemal karke background mein reels aur high-res images pre-load karti rehti hain chahe aap phone use na kar rahe hon.

### Apps Ko Restrict Karein:
- Settings ➔ **Battery** ➔ **App Battery Management** mein jayein.
- Heavy social media apps ko select karein aur unka background status **"Restricted"** ya **"Optimize"** set karein.
- Isse sirf tabhi data use hoga jab aap app ko samne kholenge.

## 6. System-Wide Dark Mode (AMOLED Screen Ke Liye Ram-Baan)

Agar aapke phone mein AMOLED display hai (jaise hamare [Best Smartphones Under ₹15,000 Guide](/blog/best-smartphones-under-15000/) mein bataye gaye phones mein hota hai), toh black background dikhane ke liye display ke sub-pixels physical power cut kar dete hain.

- System-wide **Dark Mode** on rakhein.
- Homescreen par True Black (#000000) wallpaper use karein.
- Light mode ke mukable Dark mode 5G par lagbhag **15% se 20% total battery bacha leta hai**.

## 7. Jio True 5G vs Airtel 5G: Battery Consumption Ka Farak

India ke do sabse bade telecom operators alag-alag 5G architecture use karte hain:

- **Reliance Jio (Standalone - SA):** Jio ka network pure 5G par bana hai jisme 4G tower ki help nahi lagti. Agar signal strong hai toh Jio 5G par battery efficiency better milti hai.
- **Bharti Airtel (Non-Standalone - NSA):** Airtel 4G core ke upar 5G layers chalata hai. Iska matlab hai ki aapka phone 4G signal aur 5G data dono channels ko ek sath maintain karta hai, jisse simultaneous dual-radio drain hota hai.

## 8. Battery Health Protection (80% Charging Rule)

Agar aap 67W, 80W ya 120W fast charger use karte hain aur phone ko raat bhar 100% par laga chhod dete hain, toh lithium-ion battery ki chemical life 6 mahine mein kharab ho jati hai.

- Settings ➔ **Battery** ➔ **Battery Health** mein **"Stop charging at 80%"** ya **"Optimized Battery Charging"** ko on karein.
- Phone ko 20% se neeche mat aane dein aur 85% se upar charge karne se bachein.
