Jab se India mein Jio aur Airtel ka True 5G network roll out hua hai, internet speed toh rocket ban gayi hai, lekin ek nayi musibat shuru ho gayi hai — **Battery Drain!** 

Kayi users ki complaint hai ki jo phone pehle pura din chalta tha, ab dopahar hote-hote 20% par aa jata hai. Asal mein 5G radio chip 4G ke mukable 30 se 40% zyada power khinchti hai. Lekin agar aap apne Android ya iPhone mein ye 6 settings theek kar lein, toh aapki battery pehle jaisi lambi chalne lagegi.

## 1. "Smart 5G / Auto 5G" Ko On Karein

Agar aapne phone ko hamesha "5G Only" mode par set kiya hua hai, toh jab aap WhatsApp par sirf text message padh rahe honge tab bhi phone high-power 5G antennas use karega.

### Kaise Set Karein:
* Settings ➔ **Mobile Network / SIM Cards** mein jayein.
* Preferred Network Type mein **"5G/4G/3G/2G (Auto)"** ya **"Smart 5G"** select karein.
* Isse phone jab aap YouTube ya bade downloads karenge tab 5G use karega, aur normal standby par battery bachane ke liye 4G par shift ho jayega.

## 2. Adaptive / Dynamic Screen Refresh Rate On Karein

Aajkal sabhi naye phones mein 120Hz ka smooth display aata hai, jo battery ka dusra sabse bada dushman hai.
* Settings ➔ **Display & Brightness** ➔ **Screen Refresh Rate** mein jayein.
* Ise "Always 120Hz" se hata kar **"Auto-Select / Dynamic"** par set karein.
* Jab aap reading kar rahe honge toh refresh rate khud gir kar 60Hz ya 30Hz ho jayega, jisse battery ghanto tak bachegi.

## 3. Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Scanning Ko Band Karein

Kya aapko pata hai ki phone ka Wi-Fi aur Bluetooth band hone ke bawajood background mein lagatar aas-paas ke networks ko scan karta rehta hai?
* Settings ➔ **Location** ➔ **Location Services** mein jayein.
* Wahan **Wi-Fi Scanning** aur **Bluetooth Scanning** dono ko **Turn OFF** kar dein.

## 4. Background Apps Ki Battery Optimize Karein

Instagram, Facebook, aur Games background mein chal kar battery consume karte hain.
* Settings ➔ **Battery** ➔ **App Battery Management** mein jayein.
* Jo apps aap din mein 1-2 baar hi kholte hain, unka background activity **"Restrict"** ya **"Optimized"** kar dein.

## 5. AMOLED Display Par "Dark Mode" Use Karein

Agar aapke phone mein AMOLED display hai (jaise [Best Smartphones Under 15000](/blog/best-smartphones-under-15000) mein milta hai), toh black color dikhane ke liye display ke pixels physically band ho jaate hain (0% battery consumption).
* System-wide **Dark Mode** on rakhne se daily 15-20% battery extra milti hai!

## 6. Weak 5G Signal Area Mein 4G Use Karein

Agar aap kisi aisi jagah hain (jaise basement ya gaon) jahan 5G ka sirf 1 ya 2 danda aa raha hai, toh phone tower se connect hone ke liye maximum power lagata hai aur garam hone lagta hai. Aise waqt temporary tor par network ko **LTE / 4G** par set kar dein.

## Conclusion

5G ki fast speed ka maza zaroor lein, lekin phone ki settings ko smart banayein. In 6 tips ko follow karne ke baad aapko din mein baar-baar charger dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi padegi!
