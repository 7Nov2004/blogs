Aaj kal desh bhar mein ek naye kism ka cyber aatank faila hua hai jise **"Digital Arrest"** kaha ja raha hai. Prime Minister Narendra Modi ne khud apne *Mann Ki Baat* program mein aur Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) ne public advisories ke zariye nagrikon ko is khatarnak scam se satark rehne ki appeal ki hai.

Is scam mein retired officers, teachers, homemakers aur yahan tak ki IT engineers ko target banakar unke jeevan bhar ki savings (lakho rupaye) chori kar li jati hain.

Lekin kanooni roop se sach kya hai? Aur agar aapke ya aapke parivar ke kisi sadasya ke paas aisa call aaye, toh aapko kya karna chahiye? Is comprehensive guide mein hum Digital Arrest scam ke kaam karne ke tareeqe aur khud ko protect karne ke proven steps dekhenge.

---

## 🛑 Asli Sach: Kanoon Mein "Digital Arrest" Kuch Hota Hi Nahi!

Sabse pehle yeh baat apne dimag mein saaf kar lijiye:
- **Zero Legal Validity:** Bharat ke samvidhan aur police rules ke mutabiq **Skype, WhatsApp ya Zoom par kisi ko arrest karne ka koi niyam nahi hai**.
- **No Money for Bail on Phone:** Koi bhi asli CBI officer, Police Commissioner, ya ED sleuth telephone ya online account transfer ke zariye paise, security deposit ya clearance fee nahi maangta.
- **Physical Summons Rule:** Agar kisi ke khilaf koi complaint hoti hai, toh police लिखित (written) summon bhejti hai aur physically police station bulati hai.

## 🎭 Scammers Ka Modus Operandi: Ye Trap Kaise Banta Hai?

Digital Arrest scam ka pura khel **Psychological Fear (darr aur ghabrahat)** par tika hota hai:

### Step 1: Phishing Call Se Shuruat (Courier / SIM Cut Alert)
Sabse pehle aapko ek automated robotic voice ya operator ka call aata hai:
- *"Aapka FedEx / DHL courier package Mumbai Airport par intercept hua hai jisme 5 farzi passports aur 150 gram illegal drugs mile hain."*
- Ya *"Aapke Aadhaar se jude number se illegal transactions hue hain, agle 2 ghante mein aapke sabhi telecom connections kaat diye jayenge."* (Aise farzi SIMs check karne ke liye hamara [Aadhaar Par SIM Check Guide](/blog/aapke-naam-par-kitne-sim-hai-tafcop-portal-guide/) padhein).

### Step 2: Virtual Police Station Setup Par Video Call
Jab shikar darr jata hai, toh call ko kisi "Cyber Cell Officer" ya "DCP Crime Branch" ko transfer karne ka natak kiya jata hai:
- Aapko WhatsApp ya Skype download karne ko kaha jata hai.
- Video call par samne ek vyakti police uniform, shoulder badges aur name plate lagakar baitha dikhta hai.
- Background mein police control room ki light aur walkie-talkie ki continuous audio play ho rahi hoti hai taaki wo asli lage.

### Step 3: "Digital Arrest" Ki Dhamki Aur Isolation
- Scammer shikar se kehta hai ki Supreme Court ke order ke tehat aap **"Virtual Custody / Digital Arrest"** mein hain.
- Shikar ko akele kamre mein baithne ko bola jata hai aur kisi bhi family member ya advocate se baat karne par jail bhejne ki dhamki di jati hai.
- Video camera 24 ghante on rakhne ko bola jata hai taaki victim ka dimaag panic state mein bana rahe.

### Step 4: Secret RBI Verification Account Ka Jhaansa
Aakhri step mein scammer bolta hai:
- *"Aapke bank account ka paisa terror funding se juda hai ya nahi, ise verify karne ke liye apna sara balance hamare government safe account mein transfer karein. Verification ke 1 ghante baad sara paisa wapas refund ho jayega."*
- Darr ke maare shikar apna pura fixed deposit (FD) todkar RTGS/NEFT kar deta hai, aur paise transfer hote hi scammers call cut karke gayab ho jate hain.

## 🛡️ Agar Aisa Call Aaye Toh Kya Karein? (3 Golden Rules)

Agar aapke phone par kabhi bhi Police, CBI ya Customs ke naam par video call aaye, toh in 3 rules ko follow karein:

```
[STOP] ➔ Ghabrayein nahi. Call turant DISCONNECT karein.
[THINK] ➔ Kisi bhi video call par giraftari ya police summons nahi hota.
[ACT] ➔ 1930 Helpline aur Chakshu Portal par turant report karein.
```

### 1. Call Turant Cut Karein Aur Video Par Na Aayein
Kisi bhi anjan number se aane wale WhatsApp video call ko pick hi na karein. Agar galti se uth bhi gaya hai, toh bina dare direct disconnect kar dein. Scammers kitna bhi bolein ki police bhej rahe hain, call cut karne se koi police aapke ghar nahi aayegi.

### 2. Sanchar Saathi Chakshu Portal Par Report Karein
Government of India ke DoT ne is tarah ke fraud calls ko block karne ke liye official tool launch kiya hai. Aap step-by-step padh sakte hain ki [Chakshu Portal Par Fraud Call Aur SMS Report Kaise Karein](/blog/chakshu-portal-fraud-call-sms-report-guide/).

### 3. Apne Local Police Station Ko Inform Karein
Agar aapko fir bhi shaq ho ya darr lag raha ho, toh apne shahar ke nazdiki police station jayein aur wahan ke duty officer ko wo number dikhayein. Police turant clear kar degi ki yeh scam hai.

## 🚨 Agar Galti Se Paise Transfer Ho Chuke Hain Toh?

Agar aapke parivar mein kisi ne darr ke chalte paise transfer kar diye hain:

1. **Immediate Golden Hour Action:** Agle 1 se 2 ghante ke andar **National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930** par call karein.
2. **Online Portal FIR:** Official website `cybercrime.gov.in` par jakar suspect ka mobile number, bank account details aur transaction ID ke sath online complaint submit karein.
3. **Bank Nodal Alert:** Apne bank branch manager ko inform karke unauthorized transaction ka written dispute raise karein (Dekhein hamari guide: [Wrong Transaction Refund Process RBI Rules](/blog/wrong-upi-transfer-refund-process-rbi-rules/)).
4. Aur aisi scams ke alawa Telegram par chal rahe task fraud se bachne ke liye padhein: [Telegram Job Scam Se Kaise Bachein](/blog/telegram-part-time-job-scam-se-kaise-bachein/).
