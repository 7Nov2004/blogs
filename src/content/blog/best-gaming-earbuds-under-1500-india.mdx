## Gaming Earbuds Buying Checklist

Jab bhi aap budget gaming earbuds khareedein, to ye 3 cheezein zaroor verify karein:

1. **Dedicated Game Mode Toggle:** Normal mode mein battery save hoti hai, par gaming shuru karne se pehle 3-tap karke Beast/Game Mode ON karna zaroori hai.
2. **Dual Mic / Quad Mic ENC:** Single mic wale earbuds mein teammate ko background game sound wapas echo hota hai. Quad mic ENC voice clarity double kar deta hai.
3. **Charger Compatibility:** Inhe kabhi 65W fast charger se charge na karein, 5W ya 10W standard adapter use karein taaki case ka battery life bana rahe.
