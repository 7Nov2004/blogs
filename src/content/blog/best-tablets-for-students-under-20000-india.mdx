## Tablet Kharidne Se Pehle Students In 4 Baaton Ka Dhyan Rakhein

### 1. Tablet vs Laptop: Aapki Requirement Kya Hai?
- **Tablet lein agar:** Aap medical, humanities, commerce ya UPSC ki tayyari kar rahe hain jahan heavy reading, PDF highlighting aur lecture videos dekhna main kaam hai.
- **Laptop lein agar:** Aap Computer Science, Coding, 3D modelling ya video editing kar rahe hain. Coding ke liye hamara [Best Laptops for Students 2026](/blog/best-laptops-students-2026) dekhein.

### 2. Minimum 4GB vs 6GB RAM
Android OS aur split-screen multitasking ke liye **minimum 6GB RAM** wala variant prefer karein. 4GB variant 1 saal baad heavy apps ke sath slow ho sakta hai.

### 3. Display Aspect Ratio
16:9 aspect ratio movies ke liye achha hota hai lekin vertical reading mein patla lagta hai. 7:5 (OnePlus) ya 16:10 (Samsung) reading aur typing ke liye natural rehta hai.

### 4. Audio Quality
Online classes mein tutor ki aawaz saaf sunayi dena zaroori hai. Quad speakers (4 speakers) with Dolby Atmos support hona ek massive plus point hai.
