Aaj ke digital daur mein jab hum naya SIM card khareedne jate hain, toh local mobile dukandar hamara Aadhaar card aur biometric fingerprint scan leta hai. Kai baar fraud dukandar ya cyber criminals ek hi fingerprint ko do baar scan karwake ya purane documents ka galat istemal karke **aapke naam par ek se zyada farzi (fake) SIM cards issue karwa lete hain.**

In farzi SIM cards ka istemal cyber thugs online bank fraud, OTP theft, blackmailing aur phishing scams mein karte hain. Jab police aise cases ki investigation karti hai, toh investigation seedha us vyakti tak pahunchti hai jiske naam par Aadhaar registered hota hai—**yaani bina kisi galti ke aap qanooni musibat mein phas sakte hain.**

Is gambhir samasya ko khatam karne ke liye Bharat Sarkar ke Department of Telecommunications (DoT) ne **TAFCOP (Telecom Analytics for Fraud management and Consumer Protection)** portal launch kiya hai, jo **Sanchar Saathi** initiative ka hissa hai. 

Is verified guide mein hum step-by-step dekhenge ki aap ghar baithe 2 minute mein kaise check kar sakte hain ki aapke Aadhaar par kitne SIM active hain aur anjan numbers ko online kaise block karwayein.

---

## DoT Guidelines: Ek Vyakti Par Kitne SIM Legal Hain?

Bharat Sarkar ke Telecom vibhag ke official niyamon ke anusaar SIM ownership ki limits tay hain:

* **Normal Telecom Circles (Rest of India):** Ek nagrik ke naam par maximum **9 mobile connections** allow hain (chahe woh Jio, Airtel, Vi ya BSNL kisi bhi company ke hon).
* **Sensitive Circles (J&K, Assam, North-East):** Suraksha drishti se in rajyon mein maximum **6 SIM connections** ki anumati hai.
* **Over-Limit Penalty:** Agar kisi vyakti ke naam par 9 se zyada active connections paye jate hain, toh telecom operator baki sabhi extra connections ki outgoing service suspend kar deta hai aur physical re-KYC mangta hai.

Agar aapne apne phone ki suraksha ke liye [SIM Swap Fraud Protection](/blog/sim-swap-fraud-kya-hai-kaise-bachein/) aur [Aadhaar Biometric Lock](/blog/aadhaar-biometrics-lock-unlock-maadhaar-guide/) activate nahi kiya hai, toh TAFCOP check karna aapke liye mandatory ho jata hai.

## TAFCOP Portal Par SIM Check Karne Ka Step-by-Step Process

TAFCOP portal ko use karna behad aasan hai aur iske liye kisi app ko download karne ki zaroorat nahi hai:

### Step 1: Official Sanchar Saathi Portal Kholein
Apne mobile ya computer ke browser mein official government website open karein:
* **Official URL:** `https://tafcop.sancharsaathi.gov.in`

*(Dhyan rahe kisi fake ya third-party website par apna number na dalein, hamesha `.gov.in` domain hi verify karein).*

### Step 2: 10-Digit Mobile Number Enter Karein
* Homepage par aate hi aapko **"Enter your Mobile Number"** ka box dikhega.
* Yahan apna woh primary mobile number dalein jo aapke Aadhaar card se link hai aur jise aap regular use karte hain.
* Screen par diya gaya Captcha code enter karein aur **"Validate Captcha"** par tap karein.

### Step 3: OTP Verification
* Kuch hi seconds mein Bharat Sarkar ke portal se aapke phone par ek **6-digit OTP** aayega.
* OTP enter karein aur **"Login"** par click karein.

## Dashboard Par Kya Dikhayi Dega?

Login karte hi aapke samne ek dedicated dashboard open hoga jisme likha hoga:
> *"List of Mobile Numbers registered in your name"*

Yahan aapke ID proof (Aadhaar) se jude sabhi telecom numbers list honge. Privacy protection ke liye numbers partially masked format mein dikhte hain (jaise: `98XXXXXX45`).

Har mobile number ke samne aapko 3 action options milte hain:

| Option | Kab Use Karein? | Kya Action Hoga? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Not My Number** | Yeh number aapka kabhi tha hi nahi (Farzi SIM) | DoT turant telecom operator ko notice bhejkar SIM band karwayega. |
| **Not Required** | Yeh number pehle aapka tha par ab aap ise use nahi karte | Service provider verification ke baad ise safely permanently close karega. |
| **Required** | Yeh number aap ya aapke family member abhi use kar rahe hain | Number active aur verified status mein bana rahega. |

## Anjan Number Ko Report Aur Block Kaise Karein?

Agar list mein koi aisa number dikhta hai jo aapne kabhi nahi khareeda:

1. Us number ke left side diye gaye **Checkbox** par tick karein.
2. Neeche diye gaye options mein se **"Not My Number"** select karein.
3. Page ke top ya bottom par **"Name of User"** field mein apna pura naam likhein (jo Aadhaar card par darj hai).
4. **"Report"** button par click karein.
5. Screen par ek **Ticket Reference Number (Request ID)** generate hoga. Ise screenshot lekar ya secure note karke rakh lein.

---

## Request Status Track Kaise Karein?

Report submit karne ke baad aap kisi bhi waqt apni request ka status check kar sakte hain:

1. TAFCOP portal par dobara login karein.
2. Top bar mein **"Ticket ID"** wale box mein apna reference number dalein.
3. **"Track"** par click karein.
4. Screen par dikh jayega ki aapka telecom operator (Jio/Airtel/Vi) us suspect number par kya karwai kar raha hai.

## Agar Farzi SIM Se Fraud Ho Chuka Hai Toh Kya Karein?

Agar aapko pata chalta hai ki aapke naam par chal rahe fake number se kisi ke sath financial fraud ya cybercrime kiya gaya hai, toh bina dare turant ye steps follow karein:

1. **National Cyber Crime Portal:** Turant `https://cybercrime.gov.in` par jakar incident darj karein.
2. **National Cyber Helpline:** Toll-free number **1930** par call karke complaint register karwayein.
3. **Written Police Intimation:** Apne nazdiki police station ya Cyber Cell mein ek formal application dein ki yeh number aapka nahi hai aur aapne ise TAFCOP portal par already "Not My Number" report kiya hua hai.
4. **CEIR Device Check:** Agar aapka apna handset chori ho chuka hai, toh hamari [Phone Chori Hone Par CEIR Block Guide](/blog/phone-chori-hone-par-kya-kare-ceir-portal-hindi/) padhkar IMEI number blacklist karwayein.

## Bhavishya Mein SIM Fraud Se Bachne Ke 4 Golden Rules

1. **Aadhaar Biometric Hamesha Lock Rakhein:** mAadhaar app ya UIDAI portal par jakar apna biometric fingerprint lock karke rakhein. Isse koi bhi dukandar aapke bina permission duplicate KYC nahi kar sakta.
2. **Masked Aadhaar Use Karein:** Naya SIM lete waqt regular Aadhaar ke bajaye official "Masked Aadhaar" (jisme pehle 8 digit chhupe hote hain) share karein.
3. **Har 6 Mahine Mein TAFCOP Check Karein:** Saal mein kam se kam do baar TAFCOP portal par login karke apna active number count verify karein.
4. **Purane SIM Ko De-link Karein:** Agar koi purana number band ho gaya hai, toh use apne bank account, UPI apps aur email ID se turant un-link kar dein.
