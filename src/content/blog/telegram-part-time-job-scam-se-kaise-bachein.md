## The 4-Stage Trap: Ye Scam Kaam Kaise Karta Hai?

Is fraud ka dimaag psychological manipulation par chalta hai. Iske 4 main stages hote hain:

```
Stage 1: WhatsApp Hook (Free Trust Building)
   ⬇
Stage 2: Shuruati ₹150-₹300 Ka Asli UPI Payment
   ⬇
Stage 3: Telegram "Prepaid VIP Investment" Task
   ⬇
Stage 4: Account Freeze Trap & Total Loss
```

---

### Stage 1: The Initial Contact (WhatsApp Hook)
Scammers kisi unknown foreign number (jaise +84 Vietnam, +62 Indonesia, +234 Nigeria, ya virtual +1 USA numbers) se message karte hain. 

Aapko aasan task diya jata hai: *"In 3 YouTube channels ko subscribe karo aur screenshot bhejo, aapko ₹150 milenge."*

### Stage 2: The Bait (Asli Paisa Bhejna!)
Yahan 90% log trap ho jate hain!
Jab aap screenshot bhejte hain, toh wo aapse aapka Google Pay ya PhonePe UPI mangte hain aur **sach mein aapke account mein ₹150 ya ₹200 bhej dete hain!**

Aapke dimaag mein lagta hai: *"Arrey waah! Ye toh sach mein paise de rahe hain, ye company genuine hai!"* 

### Stage 3: The Telegram VIP Shift
Ab wo aapse kehte hain: *"Daily aur zyada kamayi ke liye hamare official Telegram group aur mentor se connect karein."*

Wahan ek group hota hai jisme 50-100 log pehle se hote hain (jo sab scammers ke fake bots hote hain). Har minute koi screenshot daal raha hota hai: *"Received ₹25,000 profit!"*, *"Thanks mentor, got ₹50,000!"*

Ab mentor kehta hai:
- Task 1: Normal task (like/review)
- Task 2: **"Prepaid Crypto / Merchant Task"**
  - Aapko ₹1,000 transfer karne hain, aapko 30 minute mein ₹1,400 wapas milenge.
  - Aap darte hue ₹1,000 daalte hain, aur wo sach mein aapko ₹1,400 return kar dete hain!
  - Ab aapka greed aur trust 100% lock ho chuka hai.

### Stage 4: The Execution (Lakhon Ka Nuksaan)
Ab mentor bada amount offer karta hai:
- *"VIP Level 3 Task: Transfer ₹30,000 and get ₹45,000 return."*
- Aap jaise hi ₹30,000 bhejte hain, wo kehte hain: *"System mein error aa gaya hai, aapne galat code enter kiya. Unfreeze karne ke liye ₹50,000 aur transfer karein warna pehle ke ₹30,000 bhi doob jayenge!"*
- Darr aur desperation ke chakkar mein log doston se udhar lekar 1 lakh, 2 lakh, yahan tak ki 10-15 lakh rupaye tak transfer kar dete hain.
- Aakhir mein jab victim ke saare paise khatam ho jaate hain, toh scammer usko group se block karke gayab ho jata hai!

## Sabse Badi Galti: Scammers Ka Initial ₹200 Lena Kyun Khatarnak Hai?

Bahut se log sochte hain: *"Main shana banoonga! Shuruat ke ₹200-₹300 le loonga aur fir unhe block kar doonga. Mera kya jaata hai?"*

**YE SABSE BADI GALTI HAI!** ⚠️

Kyunki scammers jo ₹200 aapke bank account mein bhejte hain, wo unka apna paisa nahi hota. Wo kisi dusre victim ka loota hua paisa hota hai.

Jab wo victim police ya Cyber Cell mein FIR darj karwata hai, toh police us transaction chain ko follow karti hai. Jis-jis account mein wo dirty money transfer hua hota hai, bank un **sabke accounts par Cyber Lien / Freeze laga deta hai!**

Aapka pura bank account, UPI aur savings sirf ₹200 ke lalach mein freeze ho sakti hain aur aapko police station ke chakkar lagane pad sakte hain.

## Fake Job Offers Pehchanne Ke 5 Red Flags

1. **Unsolicited WhatsApp Messages:** Koi bhi reputed MNC (Google, Amazon, YouTube) WhatsApp ya Telegram par part-time jobs nahi banti.
2. **Unrealistic Daily Income:** 2 ghante video like karke koi ₹5,000 daily nahi deta. Real world mein aisi koi job exist nahi karti.
3. **Shift to Telegram:** Agar koi recruiter aapse Telegram par chat karne ko kahe, toh 99.9% wo fraud hai.
4. **Prepaid / Investment Tasks:** Agar kisi kaam ko karne ke liye pehle **aapse paise maange jayein**, toh wo job nahi balki robbery hai!
5. **Private Bank Accounts for Deposit:** Payment lene ke liye wo kisi individual person ka saving account dete hain na ki registered company account.

## Agar Aapke Sath Fraud Ho Gaya Hai Toh Kya Karein?

Agar aap ya aapka koi dost is scam ka shikar ban chuka hai, toh turant ye 3 steps uthayein:

### 1. Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline)
- Turant **1930** dial karein.
- Ye Ministry of Home Affairs ki 24x7 emergency helpline hai.
- Agar aapne pichle 2-4 ghante ke andar transfer kiya hai, toh cyber cell fraudster ke bank account ko instant freeze kar sakti hai jisse paise wapas milne ke 80% chances rehte hain.

### 2. File Online Complaint at cybercrime.gov.in
- Official government portal par jaakar:
  - UTR Numbers / Transaction IDs
  - WhatsApp chat ke screenshots
  - Fraudster ke phone numbers aur bank details
  - Pura incident detail mein submit karein.

### 3. Apne Bank Ko Inform Karein
- Apne bank ki branch ya customer care par call karke transaction ko **Fraudulent Dispute** mark karwayein aur acknowledgement number lein.
