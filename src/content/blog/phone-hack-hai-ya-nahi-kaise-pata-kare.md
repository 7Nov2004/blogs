Aajkal hamari poori zindagi smartphone ke andar hoti hai — banking apps, personal photos, WhatsApp chats aur private documents. Lekin kya aapko pata hai ki bina aapke jane koi hacker ya spy app aapke phone ka microphone, camera ya calls record kar sakta hai?

Agar aapko shaq hai ki aapka Android phone ya iPhone compromised hai, toh ghabraiye mat. Aaj hum aapko **6 dangerous signs** aur **secret dialer codes** batayenge jinse aap 2 minute mein pata kar sakte hain ki aapka phone hack hai ya nahi, aur ise turant kaise theek karein.

---

## 1. Secret Codes Se Call & SMS Forwarding Check Karein

Kayi baar hackers ya fraud karne wale aapke phone me call forwarding enable kar dete hain jisse aapke OTP aur private calls unke number par redirect ho jate hain. Ise check karne ke liye ye dialer codes use karein:

### Code 1: `*#21#` (Status Check)
* Apne phone ka Phone Dialer open karein aur type karein `*#21#` fir Call button dabayein.
* Screen par ek popup aayega jisme Voice, Data, SMS, Fax likha hoga.
* Agar har jagah **"Not Forwarded"** likha hai, toh aapka phone bilkul safe hai.
* Agar kisi bhi option ke aage koi anjaan mobile number likha hai, iska matlab aapka data us number par forward ho raha hai!

### Code 2: `##002#` (Instant Eraser Code)
* Agar aapki calls forward ho rahi hain, toh turant dial karein `##002#`.
* Yeh master code sabhi types ki unconditional aur conditional call forwarding ko turant deactivate aur delete kar deta hai.

## 2. Phone Hack Hone Ke 6 Bade Warning Signs

Agar phone ke background me koi unauthorized spyware ya tracking tool chal raha hai, toh phone ye 6 unusual signals deta hai:

### 1. Standby Par Bhi Phone Garam Hona
Jab aap phone use nahi kar rahe hote hain (pocket me ya table par rakha hai) aur fir bhi wo achanak garam ho jata hai, toh iska matlab background me koi hidden app lagataar processor aur internet use kar rahi hai.

### 2. Battery Ka Paani Ki Tarah Behna
Nayi battery hone ke bawajood agar battery backup 50% gir gaya hai, toh Settings ➔ **Battery Usage** me jayein. Agar koi aisi app sabse upar 30-40% battery consume karti dikhe jise aapne kabhi download hi nahi kiya, toh wo malware ho sakta hai.

### 3. Screen Par Green / Orange Dot Ka Aana
Android 12+ aur iPhones me screen ke top-right corner par ek **Green Dot** (Camera on hone par) ya **Orange Dot** (Microphone on hone par) dikhta hai. Agar aap camera ya voice recorder nahi chala rahe hain aur fir bhi green dot chamak raha hai, iska matlab koi app chupke se aapko sun ya dekh rahi hai!

### 4. Daily Internet Data Achanak Khatam Hona
Agar 1.5GB ya 2GB data dopahar tak bina videos dekhe khatam ho jata hai, toh koi spyware aapke phone ke photos ya audio files hacker ke cloud server par upload kar raha hota hai.

### 5. Unwanted Popups Aur Unknown Apps
Phone ki home screen par achanak ajeeb se ads aana ya app drawer me bina kisi icon wali blank app (Hidden App) ka dikhna infection ka sabse bada sign hai.

### 6. Anjaan OTPs Aur Login Alerts
Agar aapko WhatsApp, Instagram ya Google account login ke OTP bina request kiye SMS me aa rahe hain, toh koi aapke accounts me ghusne ki koshish kar raha hai.

## 3. Phone Ko Turant Safe Kaise Banayein? (Action Steps)

Agar aapko apne phone me koi suspicious activity lagti hai, toh turant ye 4 steps follow karein:

### Step 1: Device Admin Apps Check Karein
Malware apps aisi permissions le leti hain jisse unhe normal uninstall nahi kiya ja sakta:
* Settings ➔ **Security & Privacy** ➔ **Device Admin Apps** (Device Administrators) kholein.
* Yahan sirf **"Find My Device"** ya official system apps allow hone chahiye. Agar koi third-party app ho, toh turant "Deactivate" karein.

### Step 2: WhatsApp Linked Devices Audit
* WhatsApp open karein ➔ Three Dots par click karein ➔ **Linked Devices** par jayein.
* Check karein ki koi anjaan browser (jaise Chrome on Windows) login toh nahi hai. Agar hai, toh turant **"Log Out"** par click kar dein.

### Step 3: Play Protect Scan Chalayein
* Google Play Store open karein ➔ Top-right profile icon par tap karein.
* **Play Protect** par click karke **"Scan"** button dabayein. Google aapke phone me kisi bhi harmful app ko scan karke delete karne ka option dega.

### Step 4: Final Brahmaastra — Factory Data Reset
Agar koi hidden app delete nahi ho rahi hai aur phone lagataar hang ho raha hai, toh apne important contacts aur photos ka backup Google Drive par lein, aur phone ko **Factory Reset** kar dein. Reset hone ke baad phone bilkul naye jaisa safe aur fast ho jayega.
