## 💡 5 Pro Buyer Hacks: Sale Shuru Hone Se Pehle Yeh Taiyari Kar Lein!

Har saal lakho log sale ke pehle din out of stock ya payment fail hone ki wajah se deals miss kar dete hain. In 5 hacks ko follow karke aap guaranteed deal grab kar payenge:

1. **Card Details & Delivery Address Pehle Se Save Karein:**
   * Sale live hone ke waqt CVV ya OTP enter karne mein 30 second ki deri bhi deal miss karwa sakti hai. Dono apps mein delivery address aur primary payment method pehle se verify karke rakhein.
2. **Bank Cards Arrange Karein:**
   * Flipkart ke liye **Axis Bank ya ICICI Bank** credit card arrange karein.
   * Amazon ke liye **SBI (State Bank of India)** credit card taiyar rakhein. In cards par direct 10% (up to ₹1,500 - ₹5,000) instant bachat hoti hai.
3. **Wishlist Feature Ka Use Karein:**
   * Jo bhi phone ya gadget aapko kharidna hai, use abhi se apni Cart ya Wishlist mein add kar lein. Isse jaise hi deal live hogi, aapko single tap par price drop reflect ho jayega.
4. **Price History Tracker Check Karein:**
   * Kuch sellers sale se pehle price artificially badha dete hain aur sale mein normal discount dikhate hain. Hamesha *Keepa* ya *PriceBefore* jaise tools se last 30 days ka price graph verify karein.
5. **Midnight Early Access Par Login Karein:**
   * Sabse saste price cuts pehle 2 se 4 ghante ke liye hi limited stock ke sath aate hain (jaise Flipkart ki "Rush Hours" deals). Early access date ki midnight (raat 12 baje) par ready rahein.
