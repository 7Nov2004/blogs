## Gaming Headphone Kharidte Samay In 4 Baaton Ka Dhyan Rakhein:

1. **USB vs 3.5mm Jack:** Agar sirf PC ya Laptop par khelte hain toh **USB headphone** lein (Virtual 7.1 aur RGB USB par hi chalta hai). Agar Mobile phone ya iPad par khelna hai toh **3.5mm jack** zaroori hai.
2. **Earcups Cushioning:** Over-ear (jo poore kaan ko cover kare) headphones hi chunein taaki bahar ka fan ya room noise naturally block ho sake.
3. **Microphone Quality:** Game ke dauran Discord ya in-game chat mein clear voice delivery ke liye noise-isolating mic hona bohot zaroori hai.

## Final Verdict
* **PC/Laptop Gamers:** **Redgear Cosmo 7.1** aankh band karke pick kar sakte hain — value for money unbeatable hai.
* **Mobile / Multi-Device Gamers:** **Cosmic Byte GS430** ₹1,000 ke andar best option hai.
