Aadhaar card aaj hamari sabse badi pehchan hai — chahe naya SIM card lena ho, bank account kholna ho, ration lena ho, ya hotel mein check-in karna ho. Har jagah hum bina soche-samjhe apna 12-digit Aadhaar number ya fingerprint de dete hain.

Lekin kya aapne kabhi socha hai ki **kisi ne aapke bina bataye aapka Aadhaar card, biometric fingerprint ya OTP kisi galat jagah verify toh nahi kar liya?**

Kai baar cyber criminals aur fraud agents purane documents ya biometric cloning ke zariye aapke naam par illegal SIM nikal lete hain (jise rokne ke liye humne [TAFCOP Portal SIM Check Guide](/blog/aapke-naam-par-kitne-sim-hai-tafcop-portal-guide/) batayi thi) ya kisi fake loan app par KYC verify karwa lete hain.

Is gambhir khatre se bachne ke liye **UIDAI (Unique Identification Authority of India)** ne **"Aadhaar Authentication History"** feature diya hai, jisse aap apne mobile se hi pichle 6 mahine ka poora transaction log dekh sakte hain.

---

## Aadhaar Authentication History Kya Hoti Hai?

Jab bhi aapka Aadhaar kisi bhi sarkari ya private system mein verify hota hai, toh UIDAI ke server par uska ek permanent record banta hai:

* **Date & Exact Time:** Kis taareekh aur kitne baje transaction hua.
* **Authentication Modality:** Verification kaise hua — Fingerprint se, Eye (Iris) scan se, ya Mobile OTP se.
* **Auth Response Code:** Transaction Success hua ya Failed hua.
* **AUA (Authentication User Agency) Name:** Kis sanstha (Bank, Telecom Operator, ya CSC Centre) ne verification request bheji.
* **Transaction ID:** Dispute hone par police ya UIDAI complaint ke liye zaroori reference code.

## myAadhaar Portal Se History Check Karne Ka Step-by-Step Process

Aadhaar authentication log check karne ke liye aapko kisi cyber cafe ya Aadhaar centre jane ki zaroorat nahi hai:

### Step 1: Official myAadhaar Portal Kholein
* Apne mobile ya laptop browser mein official website open karein:
* **Official URL:** `https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in`
* Homepage par **"Login"** button par tap karein.

### Step 2: Aadhaar Number & OTP Se Login Karein
* Apna 12-digit Aadhaar number enter karein.
* Screen par diya gaya Captcha code dalein aur **"Login with OTP"** par click karein.
* Aapke Aadhaar-linked mobile number par 6-digit OTP aayega. OTP enter karke submit karein.

### Step 3: "Authentication History" Select Karein
* Dashboard open hone par alag-alag services ke boxes dikhenge.
* Scroll karein aur **"Authentication History"** wale option par tap karein.

### Step 4: Date Range Aur Modality Chunein
* **Select Modality:** Agar aapko sabhi transactions dekhne hain toh **"ALL"** select karein. Agar sirf biometric dekhna hai toh "Biometrics" select karein.
* **Date Range:** "Start Date" aur "End Date" chunein (maximum 6 mahine tak ka window).
* **"Fetch Authentication History"** par click karein.

Screen par pichle sabhi transactions ki detailed list table ke roop mein khul jayegi.

## Log Check Karte Waqt Kin Baaton Ka Dhyan Rakhein?

History list dekhte waqt ye 3 baatein zaroor check karein:

1. **Unknown Bank ya Telecom Transactions:** Agar aapne pichle 2 mahine mein koi naya SIM ya bank account nahi khola, lekin list mein kisi telecom company (Jio/Airtel) ya payment bank ka naam dikh raha hai, toh yeh warning sign ho sakta hai.
2. **Failed Biometric Attempts:** Agar kisi din achanak 4-5 failed biometric transactions dikhein jo aapne nahi kiye the, toh iska matlab koi aapka clone fingerprint use karne ki koshish kar raha tha.
3. **Download PDF Copy:** Page ke bottom par **"Download PDF"** ka option hota hai. Ise apne phone mein safe download karke rakh lein taaki zaroorat padne par as proof use kiya ja sake.

## Agar Suspicious Transaction Dikhe Toh Turant Kya Karein?

Agar history mein koi anjan transaction milta hai jiska aapse koi lena-dena nahi hai:

1. **Transaction ID Note Karein:** Us particular entry ki 12-digit Transaction ID aur timestamp note karein.
2. **UIDAI Toll-Free 1947:** Turant 1947 par call karein aur customer executive ko batayein ki aapke Aadhaar par unauthorized authentication hua hai.
3. **Formal Email Complaint:** `help@uidai.gov.in` par transaction screenshot aur ID ke sath mail karein.
4. **National Cyber Crime Reporting:** Agar kisi financial fraud ka andesha ho, toh turant cyber crime helpline **1930** ya `cybercrime.gov.in` par complaint darj karein.

## Bhavishya Mein Aadhaar Misuse Se Kaise Bachein?

Aadhaar ko secure rakhne ke 2 sabse aasan aur effective tarike hain:

* **Biometric Lock Activate Karein:** Jaise humne hamare [Aadhaar Biometric Lock/Unlock Guide](/blog/aadhaar-biometrics-lock-unlock-maadhaar-guide/) mein bataya tha, mAadhaar app se apne fingerprints ko digital lock laga dein. Jab tak aap app se unlock nahi karenge, koi bhi chor aapka fingerprint use nahi kar sakta.
* **TAFCOP SIM Verification:** Har 3 se 6 mahine mein hamare [TAFCOP SIM Check Guide](/blog/aapke-naam-par-kitne-sim-hai-tafcop-portal-guide/) ke zariye yeh confirm karte rahein ki aapke naam par koi anjan SIM card active na ho.
