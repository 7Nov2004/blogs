Smartphone aaj ke samay mein sirf ek baat karne ka device nahi hai. Hamare phone ke andar hamari poori zindagi band hoti hai — **Bank accounts, UPI apps (PhonePe, GPay), personal photos, family WhatsApp chats, emails aur documents.**

Jab achanak phone metro mein pocket mar li jaye, bus mein gir jaye ya market se chori ho jaye, toh sabse pehle dimaag blank ho jata hai: *"Ab mera personal data chor ke haath lag jayega, wo bank khali kar dega!"*

Pehle ke samay mein police station ke chakkar lagane padte the aur 95% cases mein phone kabhi wapas nahi milta tha. 

Lekin Bharat Sarkar ne technology ka istemal karke ek aisa portal launch kiya hai jisse aap **apne ghar baithe 5 minute ke andar chori hue phone ka IMEI number poore desh mein block karwa sakte hain — jiska naam hai CEIR (Central Equipment Identity Register) Portal.**

Is complete actionable guide mein hum dekhenge ki phone chori ya kho jane par turant kya karna chahiye aur CEIR portal par phone block aur trace kaise karwayein!

## Phone Chori Hone Par Shuruati 30 Minute Mein Ye 3 Kaam Karein!

Panic karne se pehle turant ye 3 emergency steps lijiye:

```
Step 1: Google Find My Device / Apple Find My se Data Erase karein
   ⬇
Step 2: Telecom Customer Care ko call karke SIM Block karwayein
   ⬇
Step 3: Online Police Lost Report (e-FIR) darj karein
   ⬇
Step 4: CEIR Portal par IMEI Blacklist request dalein
```

---

### Step 1: Remote Device Lock & Erase
- **Android Users:** Turant kisi doston ya laptop par `google.com/android/find` kholein. Apna Google account login karein aur **"Erase Device"** select karein taaki phone ka saara personal data remote wipe ho jaye.
- **iPhone Users:** `icloud.com/find` par jayein aur **"Mark As Lost"** enable karein.

### Step 2: SIM Card Turant Block Karwayein
Chor sabse pehle aapke SIM card se bank OTP nikalne ki koshish karta hai (jaise hamne [SIM Swap Fraud Guide](/blog/sim-swap-fraud-kya-hai-kaise-bachein/) mein samjhaya tha). 
- Apne operator ko call karein: Jio (198), Airtel (121), Vi (199).
- Apna number instant de-activate karwayein aur usi number ka naya duplicate SIM local store se issue karwa lein.

### Step 3: Online Police Complaint (e-FIR)
Ab aapko police station mein line lagane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Har state police (Delhi, UP, Maharashtra, Bihar, Karnataka) ki official website ya mobile app par **"Lost Article Report"** 2 minute mein online fill ho jati hai. Uska acknowledgment PDF download kar lein.

## CEIR Portal Kya Hai Aur Ye Chor Ko Kaise Bebas Kar Deta Hai?

**CEIR (Sanchar Saathi)** Department of Telecommunications ka ek central database hai jo India ke sabhi telecom towers se connected hai.

Jab aap CEIR par apna phone block kar dete hain:
1. Aapke phone ka **15-digit IMEI number pure India mein Blacklist** ho jata hai.
2. Ab chor chahe phone ko format kar de, root kar de, ya usme Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL kisi ka bhi naya SIM card daal le — **phone mein kabhi tower/network nahi aayega!**
3. Phone chor ke liye ek **"Plastic Ka Dabba (E-Waste)"** ban jata hai jise wo kisi ko bech bhi nahi sakta.
4. Sabse zabardast baat: Jaise hi chor phone mein koi naya SIM dalega, tower par ek silent alarm trigger hoga aur **police ko us chor ka naya phone number aur exact live tower location** mil jayegi!

## CEIR Portal Par Phone Block Karne Ka Step-by-Step Process

Aapke paas internet aur ye details honi chahiye:
- Mobile Purchase Invoice (Bill)
- Police Complaint Acknowledgement Number
- Dono IMEI numbers (IMEI 1 aur IMEI 2)
- Ek alternate phone number (OTP lene ke liye)

### Step 1: Portal Kholein
Browser mein official website open karein:
`ceir.sancharsaathi.gov.in`

### Step 2: "Block Stolen/Lost Mobile" Par Click Karein
Home page par laal rang ka icon dikhega: **"Block Stolen/Lost Mobile"**. Us par click karein.

### Step 3: Device Information Bharein
Form mein ye details enter karein:
- Device Brand (Samsung, Xiaomi, Apple, Motorola)
- Device Model
- Mobile Purchase Invoice ki photo upload karein
- Lost Place (Kahan chori hua)
- Lost Date aur State/District select karein
- Police Station aur Police Complaint Number darj karein

### Step 4: Owner Identity Details & OTP
- Apna naam, address aur ek Identity card (Aadhaar / Voter ID) upload karein.
- Ek chalu mobile number enter karke **Get OTP** par click karein.
- OTP verify karke **Submit** kar dein.

Aapko screen par ek **Request ID** mil jayegi. Is Request ID ka screenshot le lein. 24 ghante ke andar aapka device nationwide block ho jayega!

## Agar Chori Hua Phone Wapas Mil Jaye Toh Unblock Kaise Karein?

Kayi baar police chor ko pakad leti hai ya koi bhala insaan aapka phone lauta deta hai. Aise mein phone ko dobara chalu karna bilkul aasan hai:

1. CEIR website (`ceir.sancharsaathi.gov.in`) par jayein.
2. Green icon par click karein: **"Un-Block Found Mobile"**.
3. Apni **Request ID**, apna mobile number aur unblocking ka reason select karein (e.g. *"Found by Police"* ya *"Found by Self"*).
4. OTP verify karein aur Submit karein.
5. Agle kuch ghanton mein aapka phone unblock ho jayega aur aap use pehle ki tarah use kar payenge!

## Phone Chori Hone Se Pehle Ye 3 Kaam Aaj Hi Kar Lein!

Savdhani hamesha ilaaj se behtar hoti hai:

1. **IMEI Number Note Karein:** Apne dial pad par `*#06#` dial karein aur jo 15-digit number aaye use apni personal diary ya Google Keep mein note karein.
2. **Lock Screen Par Emergency Contact Display Karein:** Android aur iPhone dono mein setting hoti hai: Settings ➔ Lock Screen ➔ **Lock Screen Text** mein apne mummy ya papa ka alternate phone number likh dein taaki phone girne par koi nek dil insaan aapko call kar sake.
3. **App Lock on Financial Apps:** PhonePe, GPay, Paytm aur Netbanking par hamesha biometric fingerprint ya strong PIN laga kar rakhein. [UPI Tap & Pay NFC Security Guide](/blog/upi-tap-pay-nfc-payment-kya-hai-2026/) refer karein.
