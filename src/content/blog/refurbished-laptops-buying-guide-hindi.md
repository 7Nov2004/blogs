Aajkal naye laptops ki keemat bohot zyada ho gayi hai. Aise mein students aur budget buyers ke liye **Refurbished Laptops** ek bohot acha option ban gaye hain. Amazon aur Flipkart par aise laptops aadhi keemat par mil jaate hain.

Lekin sawal ye hai ki kya refurbished laptop lena safe hai? Kahin aapke sath scam toh nahi ho jayega? Aaj hum aapko 5 aisi baatein batayenge jo aapko koi bhi refurbished laptop lene se pehle zaroor check karni chahiye.

## Used vs Refurbished: Kya Farak Hai?

Bohot se log 'Second Hand' aur 'Refurbished' ko ek hi samajhte hain, lekin inme bada farak hai:
* **Used (Second Hand):** Ye laptop aap kisi aam insaan se direct kharidte hain (jaise OLX par). Isme koi checking ya warranty nahi hoti.
* **Refurbished:** Ye laptops companies (jaise Amazon Renewed, Cashify) ke paas aate hain. Company inke parts (jaise battery, screen) check karti hai, kharab parts ko replace karti hai aur ek choti warranty (6 mahine ya 1 saal) ke sath bechti hai.

Refurbished hamesha used se behtar hota hai kyunki isme testing aur warranty milti hai.

## 5 Cheezein Jo Zaroor Check Karein

Refurbished laptop aapke paas aate hi sabse pehle ye 5 tests zaroor karein:

### 1. Battery Health Test
Purane laptops mein sabse pehli cheez jo kharab hoti hai wo hai battery. Windows mein battery report nikalne ke liye:
1. Command Prompt (cmd) open karein.
2. Type karein: `powercfg /batteryreport` aur Enter dabayein.
3. Ek file banegi jisme 'Design Capacity' aur 'Full Charge Capacity' likha hoga. Agar 'Full Charge' capacity 'Design' capacity ke 80% se kam hai, toh laptop return kar dein.

### 2. Screen aur Dead Pixels Check
Screen par kahin koi black spot ya line toh nahi hai? Ise check karne ke liye apne browser mein **"Dead Pixel Test"** search karein. Ye website poori screen ko alag-alag colors (Red, Blue, Green, White) se bhar deti hai, jisse koi bhi kharab pixel asani se dikh jata hai.

### 3. Keyboard ke Saare Buttons Check Karein
Kayi baar refurbished laptops mein 1-2 keys kaam nahi karte aur hume dino baad pata chalta hai. Iske liye **"Keyboard Tester"** website par jayein aur har ek key daba kar check karein ki screen par green light on ho rahi hai ya nahi.

### 4. RAM aur Storage (SSD) Check
Check karein ki jo specs aapko website par dikhaye gaye the, wahi bheje gaye hain ya nahi.
* `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` daba kar Task Manager open karein.
* "Performance" tab mein jaakar check karein ki kitni RAM hai.
* Ye bhi check karein ki andar HDD (Hard Disk) hai ya SSD. 2026 mein hamesha **SSD** wala laptop hi lena chahiye, warna laptop bohot slow chalega.

### 5. Ports aur Wi-Fi Connectivity
Pen drive laga kar saare USB ports check karein. Earphones laga kar audio jack check karein. Laptop ko Wi-Fi se connect karke YouTube par ek video chala kar dekhein ki connection bar-bar drop toh nahi ho raha.

## Kahan Se Kharidna Safe Hai?

Agar aap refurbished laptop lene ka soch rahe hain, toh in trusted platforms se hi lein:
* **Amazon Renewed:** Inki return policy sabse best hoti hai. Agar laptop pasand na aaye, toh bina kisi sawal ke wapas ho jata hai.
* **Cashify:** Ye physical stores bhi chalate hain, jahan aap jaa kar khud laptop check karke le sakte hain.
* **Lenovo / Dell Outlet:** Ye companies khud apne purane laptops theek karke saste mein bechti hain aur inki warranty bilkul naye laptop jaisi hoti hai.

## Conclusion

Refurbished laptop lena ek bohot smart decision ho sakta hai, bas shart ye hai ki aapko pata ho ki kya check karna hai. Laptop aate hi sabse pehle upar bataye gaye 5 tests karein, aur agar kuch bhi kharab lage, toh turant return period ke andar wapas kar dein.
