## 🛑 Number #1 Golden Rule: OTP Kab Dena Hai?

Har e-commerce consumer ko ye rule pathar ki lakeer ki tarah yaad rakhna chahiye:

```
Parcel Arrival 
       ↓ 
Outer Carton Cut In Front of You 
       ↓ 
Brand Box Unsealed & Physical Inspection 
       ↓ 
Phone Power ON & IMEI Match with Invoice 
       ↓ 
Everything Perfect? → Tabhi OTP Share Karein!
       ↓ 
Kuch Kharab/Missing? → Refuse OTP & Reject Delivery!
```

### Pehle OTP Kyu Nahi Dena Chahiye? (The Trap Explained)
E-commerce system mein **Delivery OTP koi ordinary code nahi hai — ye aapka digital legal signature hai!**

Jaise hi delivery boy aapke muh se OTP sunkar apne app mein enter karta hai:
- System par status lag jata hai: **"Order Delivered & Inspected Successfully by Customer"**.
- Ab agar box ke andar tuta hua display nikle, charging cable gayab ho, ya duplicate model ho — toh Flipkart/Amazon ka backend algorithm bolega: *"Aapne khud check karke OTP diya tha, ab replacement nahi milegi!"*

## 📋 Open Box Delivery: Official Inspection Checklist (2026)

Jab delivery agent parcel lekar aaye, toh in 5 cheezon ko step-by-step follow karein:

| Inspection Step | Delivery Agent Ki Duty | Customer Ka Adhikar (Your Right) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Outer Brown Box** | Tape cut karke bubble wrap nikalna | Check karein ki packet pehle se tempered toh nahi tha |
| **2. Brand Seal Sticker** | Phone/Laptop box ki original seal todna | Dhyan se dekhein ki factory seal double-taped toh nahi |
| **3. Physical Hardware** | Device ko box se bahar nikalna | Screen, camera lens aur corners par scratches check karein |
| **4. Power ON Test** | Device ka power button dabana | Check karein ki display on hota hai aur koi line nahi hai |
| **5. IMEI / Serial Match** | Handheld scanner se barcode scan karna | Box ka IMEI number invoice bill se 100% match hona chahiye |

## Agar Delivery Boy Box Kholne Se Mana Kare Toh Kya Karein?

Kayi delivery executives bolte hain: *"Hume allow nahi hai, aap baad mein video banakar customer care ko bol dena."*

**Ye 100% jhooth hota hai!** Delivery partner ko har successful OBD inspection ke alawa extra incentive milta hai. Agar agent bahana banaye:

1. **Apna Phone Nikaliye Aur Recording Shuru Karein:** Delivery boy ko frame mein rakhte huye politely bolein: *"Bhaiya, ye Open Box Delivery order hai, aap company rules ke hisaab se box kholiye."*
2. **Parcel Accept Karne Se Inkaar Karein:** Saaf bol dein ki bina open box inspection ke aap parcel nahi lenge.
3. **Instant Customer Care Escalation:** Delivery boy ke khade-khade Flipkart (044-45614700) ya Amazon Support par call karein aur speaker on karke agent se baat karwayein. Customer care turant agent ko box kholne ka command deta hai.

## Open Box Delivery vs Regular 7-Day Replacement

Bohot se users confuse hote hain ki *"Agar humne OBD mein phone accept kar liya, toh kya baad mein koi problem aane par phone change hoga?"*

In dono policies ka antar samajhna zaroori hai:

- **Open Box Delivery:** Sirf **External Physical Appearance** (Cracked glass, physical dent, wrong color/model, empty box) ko check karti hai.
- **7-Day Service Center / Replacement Policy:** Agar phone accept karne ke 2 din baad Wi-Fi connect na ho, speaker aawaz na kare, ya phone restart hone lage (Internal Hardware/Software issues), toh brand service center aur e-commerce replacement policy **100% applicable** rehti hai.
