## 2. Speed & 5G Performance Test

### Reliance Jio (True 5G - Standalone)
* **Speed:** 300 Mbps se 700 Mbps (5G coverage areas mein).
* **Unlimited 5G:** Jio ke 2GB/day wale plans me Unlimited 5G free milta hai (StandAlone network ki wajah se latency kaafi kam aati hai).
* **Indoor Coverage:** 700MHz low-band spectrum ki wajah se ghar ke andar aur lifts me bhi 5G signal acche se milte hain.

### Bharti Airtel (5G Plus - Non-Standalone)
* **Speed:** 250 Mbps se 600 Mbps.
* **Calling Quality:** VoLTE aur VoNR calling quality Jio ke mukable thodi crisp aur stable feel hoti hai.
* **Gaming Ping:** Call of Duty Mobile aur BGMI me Airtel ka ping kaafi stable rehta hai (jitter kam hota hai).

### BSNL (4G Expansion & 5G Trials)
* **Speed:** 15 Mbps se 45 Mbps (4G areas mein).
* **5G Status:** Government ne Indigenous Made-in-India technology se TCS aur C-DOT ke sath milkar 1 lakh towers par 4G deploy kar diya hai jo OTA update se 5G me upgrade ho rahe hain. Metro cities me trials chal rahe hain, par har jagah fast 5G abhi nahi hai.

## 3. Kya Aapko BSNL Par Port Karwana Chahiye?

Bohot se log trend dekh kar BSNL par port karwa rahe hain, lekin baad me unhe network drop ki dikkat aati hai. Yahan samajhiye ki kisko port karna chahiye aur kisko nahi:

### ✅ In Logon Ko BSNL Par Jana Chahiye:
1. **Secondary SIM Users:** Agar aapke phone me 2 SIM hain aur dusra SIM sirf incoming calls, bank OTP aur UPI ke liye rakhte hain, toh BSNL sabse best hai.
2. **Budget Callers & Senior Citizens:** Jinhe daily gigabytes data nahi chahiye, sirf unlimited calling aur validity se matlab hai.
3. **Government Employee / Rural Areas:** Jin gaon me BSNL ka exclusive tower achi range deta hai.

### ❌ In Logon Ko BSNL Par Nahi Jana Chahiye:
1. **Hardcore Gamers:** Agar aap BGMI ya Free Fire khelte hain, toh BSNL me high ping aur packet loss mil sakta hai.
2. **Work From Home / Heavy Downloaders:** Daily 20-30GB data use karne walo ke liye Jio/Airtel ka Unlimited 5G plan zaroori hai.
3. **Travelling Executives:** Train aur highway travel me BSNL ka call drop rate abhi bhi private operators se zyada hai.

## 4. Final Verdict: Aapke Liye Kaunsa Best Hai?

* **Best Overall Value & Unlimited 5G:** 👉 **Reliance Jio** (Agar aapko sabse sasta 5G data aur accha indoor coverage chahiye).
* **Best Call Quality & Stable Gaming:** 👉 **Bharti Airtel** (Agar aap monthly ₹50-₹80 extra dekar bhi premium calling aur customer support chahte hain).
* **Best Pocket-Friendly & Validity King:** 👉 **BSNL** (Secondary SIM, parents ke phones aur affordable basic needs ke liye).

> 📲 **SIM Port Kaise Karein:** Apne current phone ke SMS app me jayein, type karein `PORT <10 Digit Mobile Number>` aur **1900** par bhej dein. Aapko ek UPC code aayega, jise kisi bhi SIM store par le ja kar aap apna number bina badle operator change kar sakte hain.
