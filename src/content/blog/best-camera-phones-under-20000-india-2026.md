India mein ₹20,000 ka budget sabse competitive segment hai. Aajkal log phone mein gaming ke sath-sath sabse zyada dhyan **Camera Quality** par dete hain — Reels banani ho, family photos leni hon ya travel vlogging karni ho.

Lekin sirf 50MP ya 108MP likh dene se camera acha nahi ho jata! Asli photo quality sensor ke size aur **OIS (Optical Image Stabilization)** par depend karti hai. Aaj hum aapko 2026 ke **Top 4 Best Camera Smartphones** batayenge jo ₹20,000 ke andar best photos nikalte hain.

## 1. Motorola Edge / G Series (Clean Camera & Natural Colors)

Motorola ne camera department mein zabardast improvement kiya hai. 
* **Camera Setup:** 50MP Sony LYT-600 sensor with OIS.
* **Kyun Lena Chahiye:** Iska color reproduction bohot natural rehta hai. Chehre ko faltu mein gora (white-wash) nahi karta aur edge detection portrait mode mein kaafi accurate hai.
* **Speciality:** Bilkul Clean Stock Android (zero bloatware aur zero ads).

## 2. Realme 13 / Narzo Pro (Portrait Aur Night Photography)

Agar aapko social-media ready photos chahiye jinme colors thode punchy aur vibrant hon, toh Realme best choice hai.
* **Camera Setup:** 50MP Main with OIS + 32MP Telephoto Portrait lens.
* **Kyun Lena Chahiye:** Is price segment mein dedicated telephoto lens milna bohot mushkil hota hai. Background blur (bokeh effect) bilkul DSLR jaisa aata hai aur low-light night mode photos crisp aati hain.

## 3. iQOO Z9 Series (Performance + Stable 4K Video)

Agar aapko camera ke sath-sath heavy gaming bhi karni hai, toh iQOO ka koi mukabla nahi.
* **Camera Setup:** 50MP Sony IMX882 sensor with OIS.
* **Video Capability:** ₹20,000 ke andar ye 4K 30fps video recording support karta hai aur OIS ki wajah se chalte-phirte video mein jhatke (shaky footage) nahi aate.
* **Performance:** MediaTek Dimensity processor ke sath gaming mein zero lag.

## 4. Redmi Note Series (Versatile All-Rounder)

Redmi ki Note series hamesha se budget buyers ki pehli pasand rahi hai.
* **Camera Setup:** High resolution sensor with AI scene enhancement.
* **Kyun Lena Chahiye:** Daytime details bohot sharp aati hain aur 120Hz AMOLED display par photos dekhne ka maza alag hi hota hai.

## Kharidne Se Pehle Ye 3 Cheezein Zaroor Dekhein

Budget camera phone lene se pehle hamesha ye check karein:
1. **OIS (Optical Image Stabilization):** Bina OIS ke raat ki photos dhundhli (blurry) aayengi. Hamesha OIS wala phone hi chunein.
2. **Sensor Brand:** Sony sensor (jaise Sony IMX882 ya LYT-600) hamesha cheap generic sensors se behtar hote hain.
3. **Display:** Photo tabhi achi dikhegi jab screen AMOLED ho.
