## Camera Phone Kharidne Se Pehle Ye 4 Rules Zaroor Janein

Dukan par sales executive ke chakkar mein aane se pehle ye technical checks zaroor karein:

### 1. The Megapixel Myth (MP vs Sensor Size)
Ek 200MP ka sensor jiska size 1/2.0" hai, wo ek 50MP ke 1/1.56" sensor se **hamesha kharab** photo dega. Bada sensor zyada light capture karta hai, jisse raat mein noise kam aur dynamic range zyada milti hai.

### 2. OIS (Optical Image Stabilization) is Non-Negotiable
Bina OIS wale phone mein sirf EIS (Electronic software cropping) hoti hai jo video ko crop karke quality gira deti hai aur raat ki photos blurry karti hai. 2026 mein bina OIS wala phone ₹15,000 se upar bilkul mat lijiye.

### 3. Dedicated ISP (Image Signal Processor)
Processor (jaise Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 ya Dimensity 7200) jitna fast hoga, camera app ka shutter utna hi fast khulega aur click karte hi zero delay mein photo process ho jayegi.

### 4. 4K Video Support Check
Bahut se brands cost-cutting ke chakkar mein processor weak dekar 4K video band kar dete hain aur sirf 1080p tak limit kar dete hain. Hamesha check karein ki phone rear camera se 4K @ 30fps record karta ho.
